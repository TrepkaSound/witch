## Which attack a creature uses

1. A youth or adult fights with its kind's own attack in the attack grammar, written for the young in `config/species-attacks.json`; the adult's is the same attack grown (one more in its pattern, areas a fifth bigger, a tenth further, lunge 1.15 times). Code: `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#speciesSpell`, `src/rules/attacks/species.ts#adultOf`, `config/tuning.json#attacks.species`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-27 (the code gives the date and a paraphrase, no quote).
2. The old attacks in `config/combat.json` are used in only two cases: the target is a soundsystem or speaker, or the link has `?attacks=old` (which sets attacks.species false). Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#c.fight?.target?.kind === "sound"`, `src/app/linkParams.ts#?attacks=old`. Ruling: none found (code only).
3. In a pack or team the grammar attack is used only when it suits the role drawn this round: a melee role uses it only if it is close (a lunge, or an area round itself); a ranged role does not use it if it is close or the pack has a designed shot of its own; an effect action never uses it. Otherwise the creature uses its pack's move. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#roleSpell`, `src/rules/combat/packs.ts#packAttack`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29 (playtest): "I didn't seem to see any projectile attacks".
4. Every grammar attack is lengthened, if needed, so its first warning comes at least 0.45 s before it can harm. Code: `src/rules/attacks/timeline.ts#liftToFloor`, `config/tuning.json#attacks.reactionFloor`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-27: "we can tune in balancing".
5. Babies never attack, are never targeted and cannot be hurt; shots and quakes pass them by. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#targetable`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#babies can't be hurt`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04: "No animals should attack babies".
6. A legend throws its kind's attack exaggerated. Its old lob or beam is used only at a soundsystem or with `?attacks=old`. Code: `src/rules/attacks/species.ts#legendOf`, `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#stepLegendAttack`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-27: "an exaggerated version of their species' attack: bigger, slower, longer range, more children".
7. Same kind never fights same kind, on any side. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#truce`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04 (date in the code, no quote).

## Teams and rounds

1. A team is every youth and adult of one side (party or wild) going for the same target, whatever their species. Legends, babies and anything attacking a soundsystem are not in a team. Teams run only with combat spacing on, which it is. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#stepTeams`, `config/packs.json#spacing.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-01 (date in the code, no quote).
2. A round goes approach, tell, combo, lull. The round is 5 s, the attack window (combo) 3 s, and at most 4 members are drawn to attack in a round; all three times are divided by the team's average tempo. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#chooseTeamActions`, `config/packs.json#spacing.team.roundTime`, `config/packs.json#spacing.team.attackWindow`, `config/packs.json#spacing.team.active`. Ruling: none found (code only).
3. The tell starts when a drawn attacker is within its action's reach plus 6 m of the target. The tell lasts the members' average rhythm tell (0.5, 0.75 or 1 s) over the tempo. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#enter("tell", w.time + tell)`, `config/packs.json#limits.engage`, `config/packs.json#rhythms.steady.tell`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. Big packs: over 20 members the round time and the lull's least rest are multiplied by (20 / size) to the power 0.5, never under 0.4. So 64 animals get 2.8 s rounds and 128 get 2 s. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#roundScale`, `config/tuning.json#teamRounds.reference`, `config/tuning.json#teamRounds.floor`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "how about we have the attack round length decrease gradually as the pack gets larger".
5. The attack window is not shortened for big packs (it stays 3 s), and the tell is never shortened. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#Math.max(scale, RT.window ?? ROUND_DEFAULT.window)`, `config/tuning.json#teamRounds.window`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04 (date in the code, no quote).
6. Roles picked ahead: a team of more than 20 keeps its next 3 rounds' attackers drawn. Those picked leave the waiting ring early so they are at their ready distance as their window opens. Nothing strikes sooner. A team of 20 or fewer draws as each round starts. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#readyBy`, `src/rules/combat.ts#SET_OFF_PACE`, `config/tuning.json#teamRounds.ahead`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "can we decide the roles in advance and have them move in sooner, in time for their attack?".
7. Waiting rings: a team of more than 20 waits in rings of 20, each ring 1.5 body gaps further out. The inner ring holds this round's unused attackers, then the coming rounds' picks, then the rest, the hurt last. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#team.order = order`, `src/rules/combatSpacing.ts#RANK_GAP`, `config/tuning.json#teamRounds.ring`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "Resolve the attack rate limit for 128".
8. Only one creature winds up or strikes at her at a time (1 token, also 1 when 12 or more are on her). One that let go within the last 0.5 s still counts. The limit holds inside packs and teams. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#const token`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.tokens`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.swarmTokens`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.gap`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "almost all animals are easily evaded simply by walking backwards, even large swarms. Ideally good play should require effective use of dodge".
9. A hurt member steps out: past its kind's withdraw share (0.55 by default) and not mid-strike, it takes the role "hurt" for at least 2 s and until its hurt is 0.2 or less. If everyone is hurt, the least hurt still attacks. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#team.withdrawn`, `config/packs.json#spacing.preferences.default.withdraw`. Ruling: none found (code only).
10. While she is in the air over hunters that are after her, their team holds in its lull and draws nothing. Her landing starts a fresh round with its approach and tell. Code: `src/rules/combat/teams.ts#members.every(c => aloftHunted(w, c, c.fight!.target))`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "I suggest that they just act how they would when they're waiting to attack".
11. At a soundsystem or speaker there are no packs, teams, combos or lulls: each creature uses its old attack at its full rate. Code: `src/rules/combat/packs.ts#tg.kind === "sound"`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-20 (date in the code, no quote).

## Waiting movement

1. A pack member waits when its pack is past the approach, it is not striking, and its attack is not ready or it is not its turn. At her, or in a team, its goal is a moving place on the team's waiting path. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#const waiting`, `src/rules/combatSpacing.ts#waitingSlot`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02: "creatures should be moving most of the time, and tend to be outside of her (default bag) range if they're not attacking".
2. The waiting path is pushed outward by (her dash reach, 15 m times 2 charges, plus 33 m) times 0.8, which is 50.4 m, plus 0.2 of any area radius beyond that. Her bag, buffs and the letters' real range do not change it. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#inviteReach: t.combatWaiting.inviteReach`, `config/tuning.json#combatWaiting.inviteReach`, `config/tuning.json#combatWaiting.reachScale`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-09: "it feels like animals move faster than invitations so it's hard to land a hit. And ranges feel small".
3. The outward push relaxes in smooth pulses, so waiters make deliberate close passes. Code: `src/rules/combatSpacing.ts#settings.passDepth`, `config/tuning.json#combatWaiting.passPower`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02 (date in docs/notes/gameplay.md, no quote for this point).
4. Waiting moves only the goal, never a body. The creature keeps its gait, its reaction delay and its turn limit, so she can dash to intercept one. Code: `src/rules/combatSpacing.ts#clearWaitingSlot`, `src/rules/combatSpacing.ts#waitingDrive`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02 (date in docs/notes/gameplay.md, no quote for this point).
5. Only this round's drawn, unused attackers ease back toward their ready distance before the window (2.5 s of lead plus their travel time). Every other waiter keeps moving on the path. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#const selected`, `config/packs.json#spacing.motion.default.returnLead`. Ruling: none found (code only).
6. Waiters steer their goal off the target's body and off a foe legend's telegraphed quake, lob, beam or blow. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#waitingHazards`, `src/rules/combatSpacing.ts#hazardAvoidance`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Blows

1. She has 4 hits. Any blow that lands costs exactly one, whatever its damage. One comes back every 20 s, and each new hit restarts that timer. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#export function hurt`, `config/tuning.json#witchHealth.hits`, `config/tuning.json#witchHealth.repairTime`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "three slashes, and then a final hit".
2. For 0.5 s after a hit, further hits are ignored. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#t.witchHealth.grace`, `config/tuning.json#witchHealth.grace`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (confirmed 0.5; no quote in the repo).
3. Nothing hits her mid-blink, knocked out, in a potion phase, before the party wakes the world, or after the night is won. Every hit test also needs her on the ground, so nothing reaches her in the air. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#export function hitWitch`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#v.onGround && !v.down`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. A blow that does not knock her out throws her straight away from it: 2.5 m, plus 0.55 m for each point of the attack's knockback, at least 12 m if the attacker rams her (a charge, a leap, a legend's run), at most 14 m. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#knockOf`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.base`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.charge`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.max`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "add a knockback and stun on the witch; make it large on chasing/ramming creatures".
5. The same blow staggers her (no moving, blinking or throwing) for 0.25 s plus 0.07 s for each metre thrown past 2.5, at most 1 s. She cannot be staggered again for 1 s after a stagger ends; blows in that time still cost a hit. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#knockWitch`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.stunBase`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.immune`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "add a knockback and stun on the witch; make it large on chasing/ramming creatures".
6. A throw passes through all scenery and she is left wherever it ends. Only a lake (on foot) or the zoo's fence stops or nudges her; trees, rocks, soundsystems, speakers and the treehouse do not. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#stepWitchKnock`, `src/rules/game.ts#blinkClear`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "There's nothing to collide with apart from lakes".
7. The Helmet takes one blow in place of a hit and is knocked off beside her; she is still thrown. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#helmetOff`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "The helmet gives you an extra hit and it's knocked off when hit".
8. At no hits she is knocked out: her hat drops where she fell, and every carried sigil falls at once on the free hex cell nearest that spot, its animal parked there and still hers. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#fallAtKnockdown`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.releaseEach`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.dropHat`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "If you're knocked down, animals' sigils fall and stay where you were knocked down".
9. Knocked out again while her hat is still on the ground: nothing new drops and the old hat stays where it lies. Code: `src/rules/hat.ts#if (!on || !h.has || h.down) return false`. Ruling: none found (code only); the file's header still marks this "DECISION FOR ED".
10. The respawn wait is a flat 6 s from going down, for every knockdown (base 6, step 0, max 6), with at least 1.5 s of scratching, then moved to the nearest bar line. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#respawnWait`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.respawn.step`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.respawn.max`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "any more than about six seconds to wait will be frustrating"; the flat wait is an undated quote in `config/knobs/witch.json#We can remove the extra knockdown time`.

## Effects on her

1. A creature's stun on her is not a stagger: it is a slow to 40% of her speed for 3 times the stun's length (release 56, PR #2073). `?stun=old` gives the old stagger. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#export function stunWitch`, `config/tuning.json#witch.stunAsSlow.speed`, `config/tuning.json#witch.stunAsSlow.time`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "I think stun and slow are very similar, and stun is less fun than slow, so how about stun is replaced with slow and we use circling stars?".
2. That change is only for stuns creatures put on her. Her letters' and her party's stuns on creatures, and every blow's own short stagger, are as they were. Code: `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#if (e.stun) w.stunWitch`, `src/rules/knock.ts#k.stunUntil = time + stun`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Big: 40% speed, 3× as long".
3. A blink dodges a stun-slow, and none lands while she is knocked out, in a potion phase or after the party is over. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#dashing(W.dash, witchTime(g, W))`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. Slows never stack. A new slow keeps the later end time; it takes the new speed, or the slower of the two if the old slow would have lasted longer. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#slowWitch: (id, until, mult)`. Ruling: none found (code only).
5. A root on her is a slow to 5% of her speed for the root's length. Code: `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#if (e.root) w.slowWitch`. Ruling: none found (code only).
6. An effect that touches her with no damage (a slowing puddle, a trail) does not cost a hit. Code: `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#if (damage > 0)`. Ruling: none found (code only).
7. Each effect on her is shown for exactly as long as it lasts, with a mark, a tint on her sprite and a sound: slowed, 3 stars circling her head and drag marks behind her steps; confused, a spiral over her head; thrown 4 m or more, a dust skid. `?effectmarks=0` turns them off. Code: `src/render/herEffects.ts#HER_EFFECTS`, `config/tuning.json#effectMarks.slow.stars`, `config/tuning.json#effectMarks.thrown.minMetres`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Mark + tint + sound".
8. The marks are coloured by an attacker's element, but the rules keep no record of who did it: the view picks the nearest creature hunting her within 45 m whose attacks can cause that effect, else the nearest hunter. Code: `src/render/herEffects.ts#effectElement`. Ruling: none found (code only).
9. A blow's own stagger has no mark of its own, only her wobble as before. An ordinary 2.5 m throw leaves no skid. Code: `src/render/herEffects.ts#has no mark of its own`, `config/tuning.json#effectMarks.thrown.minMetres`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Mark + tint + sound".

## Lunges and telegraphs

1. A melee attack whose own lunge is 8 m or more is a long lunge (not at a soundsystem, not a legend's, not a pack member striking with its own charge, leap or burrow). Its wind-up may begin 1.5 dashes from the target and the lunge runs 2 dashes along its line, through and past where it aimed. Both are scaled by lunge / 16, held between 0.75 and 1: with the 15 m dash, 16.9 to 22.5 m off and 22.5 to 30 m long. `?lunge=old` turns it off. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#export function longLunge`, `config/tuning.json#lunge.from`, `config/tuning.json#lunge.through`, `config/tuning.json#lunge.least`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Lunge ranges seem very short; they could be twice as long, around 1.5-2 dashes range".
2. A long lunge's wind-up stops and crouches where it began. Any other melee wind-up at her surges in at 1.15 times its sprint. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#else if (DA && !long && A.delivery === "melee"`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.surge`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Both".
3. A long lunge's blow lands once, as it meets her on the way; it then keeps its line to the end and never turns back. A blink through it makes it miss. An ordinary lunge's blow lands at the lunge's end if she is in reach. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#const meets = L.through ? !L.landed : L.left <= 1e-6`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Lunge ranges seem very short; they could be twice as long, around 1.5-2 dashes range".
4. An ordinary lunge at her reaches 1.4 times the attack's lunge. With momentum it goes right to where it aimed, up to 1.25 times that reach; without, it stops half its range short. A dug-in creature does not lunge. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#export function lungeOf`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.lunge`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-27 (date in the code, no quote).
5. A melee wind-up at her tracks where she will be until 0.18 s before it lets go; then its aim is locked. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#time < f.windupUntil - DA.commit`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.commit`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "almost all animals are easily evaded simply by walking backwards, even large swarms. Ideally good play should require effective use of dodge".
6. strikeTurn: a released lunge may still turn toward its target, at most its kind's strikeTurn in degrees a second (90 by default; hare 120, lynx 150, ram 35). Its travel length is fixed at release. Tracking her is allowed; how much is a balance number. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#waitingProfile(c).strikeTurn / FIGHT.momentum`, `config/packs.json#spacing.motion.default.strikeTurn`, `config/packs.json#spacing.motion.species.ram.strikeTurn`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02 (date in the code, no quote).
7. A lunge's warning is a front of dots that runs out from the creature, starting as the wind-up starts and reaching the far edge at release, with no solid fill. Its length is the lunge's real length and its width grows with strikeTurn. Code: `src/render/leash/combat.ts#lungeTell`, `src/rules/combat.ts#export function lungeLength`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02: "the end of the line is where the lunge lands".
8. Telegraph colour: a wild creature's warning shapes are drawn in its element's telegraph colour, not red (a trial; the shapes and timings did not change). Red is used only with `?telegraphs=red` or when there is no creature to dress it. Code: `src/render/leash/attackLook.ts#TELEGRAPHS_RED`, `src/render/leash/attackLook.ts#danger(d: Dress | null)`, `config/attack-look.json#elements.fire.telegraph`, `config/tuning.json#attackFx.telegraph.gain`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Let's try element colours and see".
9. Only the wild's telegraphs are drawn; a party animal's attacks show no warning shapes. Code: `src/render/leash/attackSpells.ts#telegraphs: the wild's only`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Shot marks

1. A shot mark is a small mark left on the ground where a wild creature's shot ends without leaving anything of its own: a bolt or roller that missed and ran out, or a lob landing. Her party's shots leave none. `?shotmarks=0` turns them off. Code: `src/rules/attacks/marks.ts#leaveMark`, `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#shotEnded`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Mark the ground".
2. A mark lasts 2.5 s and is 1.5 m in radius. At most 24 exist at once. A shot ending within 1.5 m of a mark of its own side renews that mark. Code: `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.time`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.radius`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.max`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.merge`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Mark the ground".
3. What a mark does goes by the shot's own effect first (a burn hurts, a slow or root slows), else by its element: fire, storm, venom and moonlight hurt; frost, water and shadow slow; earth, sand, wood, wind and bloom are rough ground. Code: `src/rules/attacks/marks.ts#markKind`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.kinds.fire`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. A hurting mark bites only someone who has stood in it for 1 s without leaving, once per mark, for 0.25 of the shot's damage. For her that is one hit. Walking through costs nothing. Code: `src/rules/attacks/engine.ts#M.stand`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.stand`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.damage`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Mark the ground".
5. A slowing mark holds her at 0.6 of her speed and rough ground at 0.8, only while she is in it; neither costs a hit. Code: `src/rules/attacks/marks.ts#MARK_TICK`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.slow`, `config/tuning.json#shotMarks.rough`. Ruling: none found (code only).
6. The old pack bolts and lobs leave marks too, except a lob that leaves its own patch (the toad's spores). Code: `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#oldShotMark`, `src/rules/combat.ts#if (sh.patch)`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Blink-dodging and ranged aim

1. A blink takes her 15 m and she cannot be hit for 0.2 s (the longer of dash.gone 0.05 and dodge.a.gone 0.2). Code: `src/rules/dash.ts#blinkGone`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.gone`, `config/tuning.json#dash.distance`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "almost all animals are easily evaded simply by walking backwards, even large swarms. Ideally good play should require effective use of dodge".
2. A ranged attack at her follows her through its wind-up, aiming where she will be when it arrives. It locks at least 0.2 s before release and at least 0.75 s (young) or 0.6 s (adult, legend) before it can reach her; an area put down on her locks 0.7 s ahead. The caster holds still from the lock. `?ranged=old` turns it off. Code: `src/rules/attacks/aim.ts#trackFor`, `config/tuning.json#ranged.lock.young`, `config/tuning.json#ranged.commit`, `config/tuning.json#ranged.areaLock`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "the preparation time seems long, projectile speed is low, if you're moving at all they basically never hit you".
3. Straight bolts and rollers at her fly at least 2 times her ground speed (38.5 m/s). Homing shots, spirals, boomerangs and beams are left out of both rules. Code: `src/rules/attacks/aim.ts#withSpeedFloor`, `src/rules/attacks/aim.ts#aimClass`, `config/tuning.json#ranged.speedFloor`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "when the changes are decided, apply them to all creatures, not just home ones".
4. A shot or lob at her reaches 2 times as far, and every ranged attack (shot, lob, beam) 1.2 times as far at anything. A shooter still stands at its usual distance. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#const reachOf`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.b.range`, `config/tuning.json#difficulty.rangedRange`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "Animals +20% speed, ranged attacks have +20% range, adults are stronger.".
5. For 0.8 s after a blink, a pack shooter's old bolt at her aims at where the blink put her. Code: `src/rules/combat/packs.ts#export function dodgeAim`. Ruling: none found (code only); the code credits Ed with no date.

## Shelter

1. She is sheltered while on the ground inside the circle of a calm legend (asleep or restless; not angry, not happy, not leashed). Nothing but that circle's own legend may target her. The code comment credits Ed, "round 14", with no date. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#export const sheltered`, `src/rules/slowTime.ts#export const calm`, `src/rules/game.ts#calmRingAt(g, w.body.x, w.body.z)?.id`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. While she is sheltered nothing from outside lands: not a blow or shot already on its way, not a charge's touch, not an effect, a pull or a push, not a slowing trail. Code: `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#if (sheltered(w.witches[tg.id], from)) return`, `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#shelteredTouch`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Who targets whom, and when they give up

1. A wild creature goes for a witch who is on the ground in its area once its watch of her is over (4 s). Across its edge it starts on her only inside its chase band (30 m past the edge) and only if a letter of hers landed on it in the last 6 s. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#export function acquire`, `config/tuning.json#wildWatch.provoked`, `config/tuning.json#combat.leaveArea`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-13: "Animals should chase outside, and relatively far. The idea is to stop people just sitting near the edge of a territory and sniping/kiting. However if you leave the area before the warning ends then they don't attack".
2. Once a wild creature is after her, it follows her anywhere on the ground, with no distance limit. The old rule (lost when out of its area, out of range and 45 m away) now applies only to besiegers. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#if (!c.siege) return true`, `config/tuning.json#combat.witchLose`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (date in the code, no quote).
3. When she takes off, a wild pursuer that is not hunting her loses her at once, gives up and walks home; it roams again once inside its area and within 40 m of home, or after 30 s. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#export function gaveUp`, `src/rules/combat.ts#c.retreat = true`, `config/tuning.json#combat.retreatHome`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "instead they should retreat and go back to idling".
4. A hunter (its area's awake wild youth or adult, once the watch is over) keeps her as its target while she is in the air over its area: it waits round the point under her and never strikes. If she is in the air outside its area, or is knocked out, it gives up and walks home. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#export function aloftHunted`, `src/rules/hunt.ts#stepHunts`.
