# Rules now: creatures and babies

## Levels

1. There are four levels: 0 baby, 1 youth (the code says "young"), 2 adult, 3 legend. Code: `src/rules/creatureLevels.ts#LEGEND = 3`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#0 baby, 1 young, 2 adult, 3 legend`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. Only her leashed animals grow, and only from berries she feeds them. One berry makes a baby a youth, and growth stops at adult: no animal is ever grown into a legend. Code: `src/rules/berries.ts#toEvolve`, `src/rules/berries.ts#TOP_LEVEL`, `config/tuning.json#berries.babyCost`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "something she does deliberately and accurately under time pressure" (feeding); Ed, 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-04 for the one berry and the stop at adult (no quote in the repo).
3. A flask is drunk where it lies: every animal on her stack that is younger than an adult becomes an adult at once. Parked animals do not. Code: `src/rules/berries.ts#growthSpurt`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#flaskUnder`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18: "All flasks give growth spurt (all creatures following you become adults)".

## How many, and where they are made

1. Every creature is made once, when the night's map is made. None is made or removed during a night: growth is off, and the game never calls the growth code. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#spawnCreatures`, `config/tuning.json#population.growth.on`, `src/rules/growth.ts#growWave`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "during a night, no creatures are created and none are destroyed".
2. The home area has no wild creatures and no area legend. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#if (home) continue;`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "Home area shouldn't have a legend".
3. Every other area starts with 2 babies (the cap is also 2) and at least 1 youth or adult. Code: `src/rules/growth.ts#routePopulation`, `config/tuning.json#population.byRoute.babies`, `config/tuning.json#population.byRoute.babyCap`, `config/tuning.json#population.byRoute.minHostile`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "every wild area has at least one hostile wild creature".
4. The number of youths and adults in an area comes from its place on the waves' route: later areas hold more and older ones. Code: `src/rules/growth.ts#routePopulation`, `src/rules/growth.ts#islandReach`, `config/tuning.json#population.swarm`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "pre-populate every area by its route position".
5. Only the areas the map picks have a legend. It sleeps in its circle, and a wild baby of its own kind is made in that circle with it. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#legendSpot`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#B.circle = circle`, `config/tuning.json#legends.share`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Legend circles should spawn with a ... baby in them, which tries to stay within the circle".
6. On an island that is not the home island, one extra happy legend of the island's kind guards the home area. It has no quest and no circle baby, and it can never be invited or picked up. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#homeGuard: true`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.homeGuard) return false;`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the repo).
7. Idle wild youths and adults keep within 35 m of their area's runestone, at least 5 m off it. Babies roam the whole area. No idle creature leaves its own area. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#idlesAtStone`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#stepCreature`, `config/tuning.json#stoneIdle.radius`, `config/tuning.json#stoneIdle.clear`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08 (the quote is in `src/rules/creatures.ts#idlesAtStone`).

## States

1. A creature is wild, happy, leashed or enraged. The code reads it in that order of checks: leashed first, then happy, then enraged, else wild. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#stateOf`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#CREATURE_STATES`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "Nobody is being killed in this game; we are throwing a party, we're inviting everyone, some people don't get invites and so are offended."
2. Leashed ones fight wild and enraged ones. Enraged ones fight happy ones. Wild and happy ignore each other, and so do wild and enraged. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#foes`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (issue #87, no quote in the repo).
3. Happy is for good. A happy creature is never enraged, is healed, and stays in its own area. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#befriend`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#enrage`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (issue #87, no quote in the repo).
4. When an area gets its soundsystem, its remaining wild youths and adults turn enraged and besiege it. Babies, legends, leashed, happy, stunned and friendly-area creatures are never enraged this way. Code: `src/rules/combat.ts#startSiege`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#enrage`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (issue #87, no quote in the repo).
5. A wild baby turns happy the moment its area gets a soundsystem (her spell or the wave), the circle baby too. That is the only way a baby turns happy. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#Its wild babies turn happy at once`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "An area should transition early when I invite all of the hostile creatures (youths and adults). Wild babies don't count; they should become party animals when the transition happens".
6. If that soundsystem later falls, the area's happy babies run off for good. Leashed and parked ones stay hers, and so does one whose sigil still lies loose on the ground. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#Its happy babies run off, for good`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (no quote in the repo).
7. Nothing can attack or hurt a baby. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#targetable`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#babies can't be hurt`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04: "No animals should attack babies".
8. A wild youth or adult whose ring is full is stunned for 20 s: it lies still and nothing attacks it. If she does not convert it in that time it gets up again, still wild. It does not run off. Code: `src/rules/orbit.ts#stun`, `src/rules/orbit.ts#getUp`, `src/rules/combat.ts#getUp(c, time)`, `config/tuning.json#combat.daze`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "make it and I'll test in gameplay" (combat as ring slots); the 20 s is Ed, 2026-10-06.
9. An enraged creature whose ring is filled by blows runs away for good. So does one of hers, or a happy one, whose ring fills with drops; one of hers is then lost for the night. A runaway is not deleted: it runs to the beach and sleeps there, gone for the rules. Code: `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#runAway(o`, `src/rules/orbit.ts#addDrops`, `src/rules/runaway.ts#runOff`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04: "it's sad when animals die".
10. Placed (parked): a leashed animal whose sigil is on the ground roams within 8 m of a spot just north of the sigil. Parked in the home area, it roams the whole home area. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#stepLeashed`, `src/rules/leash.ts#roamHome`, `config/tuning.json#leash.length`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "Animals parked in the home area can roam it freely".
11. If she is knocked out, every sigil on her stack falls onto the ground where she went down and stays there. Those animals are parked, still hers. A party legend is let go instead. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#fallAtKnockdown`, `src/rules/leash.ts#fallSpot`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.releaseEach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "If you're knocked down, animals' sigils fall and stay where you were knocked down".
12. Before the party spell is cast, and after the party is over, every creature is asleep in its own area: no fights, no rune, no invites. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#worldAsleep`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.asleep || c.bed) return false;`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "If you leave the treehouse before starting the party, it should be the same as after a party is lost; creatures are asleep in their areas and you can't invite them, the night's permanent upgrade find is not placed yet".

## Who hunts her, and when they give up

1. Landing on the ground in a wild area starts its watch: its youths and adults stare for 4 s, then attack. If she walks out or takes off before the 4 s are up, it is called off and nobody follows. Code: `src/rules/wildWatch.ts#stepWildWatch`, `config/tuning.json#wildWatch.time`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "Six second dormant period should be four seconds".
2. Only an area's own awake wild youths and adults hunt. Babies, legends, the circle baby, friendly-area creatures, statues, and enraged, stunned or sleeping ones never hunt. Code: `src/rules/hunt.ts#hunter`, `src/rules/clear.ts#holdsArea`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "all of the wild creatures from that area should fight with me until either I die or they are invited".
3. A hunt starts only for a witch on the ground in a wild area whose watch is over. It does not start while she is seated, up in the treehouse, knocked out, hidden by Stealth, or in home, a partied area or a friendly area. Code: `src/rules/hunt.ts#stepHunts`, `config/tuning.json#hunt.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07 (the quote in line 2).
4. While she stays on the ground, hunters follow her anywhere, also outside their own area. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#valid`, `src/rules/hunt.ts#stepHunts`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the repo).
5. While she is in the air over the hunters' own area, the hunt holds: they wait round the point under her and come on again when she lands. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#aloftHunted`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "I suggest that they just act how they would when they're waiting to attack".
6. When she is in the air outside the hunters' area, every hunter gives up and walks home, and that area's watch plays again on her next visit. Code: `src/rules/hunt.ts#g.wildEntry.delete(cellKey(c.cell)); c.retreat = true`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the repo).
7. A hunt also ends for one hunter when it is won over, enraged, stunned, running off or gone, and for all of a witch's hunters when she is knocked out. With Stealth it ends when she is more than 25 m away. Code: `src/rules/hunt.ts#stepHunts`, `config/tuning.json#spells.stealth.range`. Ruling: none found (code only).
8. A sleeping or restless legend is scenery: nothing touches it and it attacks nobody. It turns restless when none of its kind is in its area (checked every 5 s), and angry after 60 s of that; the circle baby, or one of its kind parked there, counts. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#dormant`, `src/rules/legends.ts#stepLegendStates`, `src/rules/legends.ts#hasKin`, `config/legends.json#angryAfter`, `config/legends.json#check`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (the redesign, no quote in the repo).

## Wild babies

1. A wild baby with a fight within 30 m runs to a spot in its own area about 55 m (up to a quarter more) from the fight. It never leaves its area. It goes back to roaming 3 s after the fight is over. Code: `src/rules/wildBabies.ts#stepWildBabies`, `src/rules/wildBabies.ts#spotFor`, `config/tuning.json#combat.defendRadius`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.scatter`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.calm`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "wild babies should avoid combat in the way that friendly babies do".
2. This does not apply to the circle baby, a napping baby, a happy baby, or a statue. Code: `src/rules/wildBabies.ts#shyBaby`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13 (the quote in line 1).
3. Babies never hold an area. She can cast at a stone while its wild babies and its sleeping legend are still there. Code: `src/rules/clear.ts#holdsArea`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#hostileSigil`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07 (the quote in States, line 5).
4. The circle baby keeps to its legend's circle, wild or happy, and walks back in if it is pushed out. Only leashed does it leave to follow her. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#keepsToCircle`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Happy Circle baby should stay in its circle, though it can dance there".

## Babies on her leash

1. A baby joins her only when she picks up its loose sigil after its area's stone has converted. It is never invited. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#Soundsystem loose-sigil pickup only; never a baby invitation`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31: "No, no baby is invitable".
2. While she is on the ground her babies walk in single file along where she has been, the baby at the bottom of her stack first. Off the ground there is no queue and no scatter. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#queueDistances`, `src/rules/babies.ts#trailPoint`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11 (no quote in the repo).
3. Neighbours in the queue keep half the front one's length, half their own and 1 m between their middles, never less than 5 m. The first baby keeps the same off her, with 1.75 m as her half. Lengths come from the meshes where a kind has one. Code: `src/rules/babies.ts#queueGap`, `src/rules/babies.ts#queueHalf`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.gap`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.margin`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.her`, `config/mesh-lengths.json#lengths`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "The spacing in this queue should adjust depending on how large the babies are (some are quite large and so smush together in the queue)".
4. When a wild creature within 30 m goes for her or one of hers, her babies scatter 55 m (up to a quarter more, plus half their length) off to one side. The direction is fixed for the whole fight. They come back 3 s after the last attack, and never run off the island's land. Code: `src/rules/babies.ts#scatterSpot`, `src/rules/babies.ts#attackerNear`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#BABY_COAST`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.scatter`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.calm`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "they should scatter further, e.g. outside of my camera view".
5. A baby has no chain and never slows her or holds her down. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#chained`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03 (the chain rework; no quote about babies in the repo).

## Followers in the treehouse

1. While she is up in the treehouse and not knocked out, every creature on her stack (babies too) acts as if parked at its own spot in the home area: it goes there, rests there and defends the whole home area. A party legend stays where it is. Code: `src/rules/houseGuard.ts#inHouse`, `src/rules/houseGuard.ts#houseGuards`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-24: "when she's in the treehouse, her followers should switch to idling in the home area".
2. No sigil is put down and her stack does not change. When she leaves the treehouse they follow her again. Code: `src/rules/houseGuard.ts#leashView`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-24 (the quote in line 1).

## Benched kinds

1. Three kinds are on the bench: marten, newt and moth. Their art, voices and attacks stay in the repo, but they have no island, so they are never dealt and nobody meets them. Code: `src/rules/kinds.ts#BENCH`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "marten is on the bench"; Ed, 2026-11-13: "can we actually rename it in the files and put the moth on the bench?".
2. The bee stands in the marten's place, the turtle in the newt's, the butterfly in the moth's. Wherever kinds are sorted by name the new kind sorts as the old one, so every seed's map and deals stay as they were. Code: `src/rules/kinds.ts#PLACE_OF`, `src/rules/kinds.ts#byPlace`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-11: "keep both, but swap the marten for the bee"; Ed, 2026-11-12: "newt for turtle".
3. Only the moth was renamed: an old save or link that says moth is read as butterfly, and the butterfly keeps the moth's seeded looks and sounds. A save that names the marten is not turned into the bee. Code: `art/creatures/renamed.js#RENAMED`, `src/rules/kinds.ts#kindNow`, `src/rules/kinds.ts#seedName`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "make a new ID, keep the marten distinct, we may want it back one day".

## Naps

1. An idle wild creature (any level but a legend) may lie down at each pause in its roam: a 30% chance, for 20 to 60 s. It only does this in an area with no soundsystem, and never while a witch is on the ground in that area. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#napper`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#stepCreaturesNear`, `src/rules/game.ts#napRules`, `config/tuning.json#naps.chance`, `config/tuning.json#naps.length`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "I think animals in wild areas which are idling can sleep. They awake when you are there in ground mode, but stay asleep if you're in treetop mode, or not in their area".
2. A napper gets up when its time is over, when a witch is on the ground in its area, when its area gets a soundsystem, or when it may nap no longer. Getting up takes 1.2 s, and it is out of fights until then. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#wakeUp`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#napping`, `config/tuning.json#naps.wake`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (the quote in line 1).
3. A letter that reaches a napping youth or adult wakes it at once and counts as a hit, and it wakes the area's other youths and adults with no getting-up time. A napping baby is not woken: the letter passes it. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#const napper`, `src/rules/inviteWakes.ts#wakeOnInvites`, `src/rules/game.ts#no baby invitation, wake or meter`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "Letters wake them"; Ed, 2026-10-31: "No, no baby is invitable".
4. The circle baby naps far more: an 85% chance, for 30 to 80 s. Only a witch on the ground within 4 m of its circle's edge wakes it, not one elsewhere in its area. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#atCircle`, `config/tuning.json#naps.circle.chance`, `config/tuning.json#naps.circle.length`, `config/tuning.json#naps.circle.reach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "the legend circle baby should spend most of its time napping when in the circle".
5. The sleep before the party and after it is over is not a nap. It has no end time, letters pass such a sleeper, and only the party's start gets it up. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#putToBed`, `src/rules/game.ts#One asleep for good`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26 (the quote in States, line 12).
