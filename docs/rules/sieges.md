# Rules now: sieges and soundsystems

## What a soundsystem is

1. Every area but home has one soundsystem spot. Its rune stone stands there (a great stump, `runeMarkers.stump.on` true) until the area converts; then the soundsystem rises on the same spot. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#soundsystemFor`, `src/rules/party.ts#spawnMarkers`, `config/tuning.json#runeMarkers.stump.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "we could make them all treestumps".
2. A soundsystem has 150 health and a 2.5 m body. Code: `src/rules/combat/siege.ts#startSiege`, `config/tuning.json#combat.soundsystemHealth`, `config/tuning.json#combat.soundsystemRadius`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "soundsystem health should be reduced substantially. I think ttk for soundsystems should be relatively short, otherwise if you're marginally losing it can take a very long time to fully lose."
3. Home has no soundsystem. Home is its ring of 12 dancefloor speakers, each its own soundsystem in the fight (keys `home:0` to `home:11`), 150 health and a 2 m body each. They are in the fight from the start of the night. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#speakerKey(i)`, `src/rules/speakers.ts#isHomeKey`, `config/tuning.json#combat.speakerHealth`, `config/tuning.json#dancefloor.speakers.count`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Home speakers should change to 150 as well".
4. An area converts (gets its soundsystem) in two ways only: a wave reaches its stone, or she casts the soundsystem spell on it. `cast.on` is true, so an area does not convert by itself when its last wild creature is invited. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#spreadWave`, `src/rules/party.ts#clearArea`, `src/rules/game.ts#if (!still && !castOn(t)) stepClear(g)`, `config/tuning.json#cast.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "It's a spell that you're choosing to cast on this stone, rather than a property of the area/stone itself."
5. A legend area has no stone of its own. It counts as a party area when its pair does. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#partifiedKey`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "The pair partifies together".

## The party spell and the boot

1. Nothing starts until she casts the party spell. Before it the world is asleep: nothing fights, nothing can be invited, and the waves wait. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#castPartySpell`, `src/rules/partyOver.ts#worldAsleep`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "If you leave the treehouse before starting the party, it should be the same as after a party is lost; creatures are asleep in their areas and you can't invite them, the night's permanent upgrade find is not placed yet".
2. The cast holds her still for 1.2 s. The boot starts only when she leaves her decks. While she sits there the boot and the first wave both wait. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#PARTY_CAST`, `src/rules/party.ts#heldBySpell`, `src/rules/party.ts#p.bootFrom === undefined && p.spellAt !== null && !seated`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "three seconds after you leave your decks".
3. The boot's pulse runs from the treehouse to the speaker ring and once round it clockwise at 4 m/s. That is its own speed, not the waves'. Each stone turns into a speaker as the pulse passes it. The first wave's pulse sets off when the boot ends. Code: `src/rules/bootRing.ts#bootSeconds`, `src/rules/bootRing.ts#stoneTurned`, `src/rules/party.ts#resetPulse(p, map, boot + map.tuning.party.startDelay)`, `config/tuning.json#boot.speed`, `config/tuning.json#party.startDelay`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "the pulse should go around the dancefloor speaker/runestone ring clockwise, starting from the top".

## Waves and the countdown

1. There is no wave timer. A wave lands when the ley pulse reaches the next stone on the route, and the countdown is the metres left divided by the pulse's speed. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#stepParty`, `src/rules/party.ts#waveCountdown`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-09: "Pure constant speed".
2. The pulse's speed follows the tempo in a straight line: 1 m/s at 100 bpm, 5 m/s at 200 bpm, times `leyLines.pulseSpeed` (1). At the tuning's 120 bpm that is 1.8 m/s. The record she puts on at her decks can change the night's tempo. Code: `src/rules/beat.ts#sparkSpeedAt`, `src/rules/beat.ts#tempoRate`, `src/rules/pulseRoute.ts#pulseSpeed`, `config/tuning.json#leyLines.pulseAt100`, `config/tuning.json#leyLines.pulseAt200`, `config/tuning.json#beat.bpm`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "a smooth increase from 100 bpm 1.0 m/s to 200 bpm 5.0 m/s".
3. The tempo is the same all night. Only her knockdowns raise it: 5 bpm each, with no cap. So every knockdown makes the waves come sooner. Code: `src/rules/beat.ts#knockdownTempo`, `src/rules/game.ts#knockdownTempo(g.beat, t, g.clock.time)`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.bpmStep`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "the tempo (pulse speed) should stay the same through the night, and only raises when you get knocked down"; the 5 is Ed, 2026-11-16: "5 BPM: keep it".
4. Losing a soundsystem or a home speaker changes nothing in the countdown or the tempo. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#loseSoundsystem`, `src/rules/party.ts#pulseLinksIn`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "Losing a soundsystem no longer touches the wave countdown".
5. Each wave wakes `party.areasPerWave` (1) area, however many witches play: the next stone on the planned route (`party.picker` is "route"). The next stone and the one after are fixed in advance. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#pickNext`, `src/rules/party.ts#planAhead`, `config/tuning.json#party.areasPerWave`, `config/tuning.json#party.picker`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "each wave still targets the next stone on the route"; Ed, 2026-11-16: "One area a wave, always" (however many witches play; this retires the 2026-10-04 one per witch).
6. A wave that reaches an area she already converted only celebrates. No second soundsystem, nobody enraged. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#p.ahead?.delete(k)`, `src/rules/game.ts#waveCelebrate`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "no enrage, no second soundsystem".
7. A ruined area (its soundsystem destroyed) is never woken again. If its wave had not come yet, the route still stops at its stone and that wave does nothing there. Code: `src/rules/party.ts#const passed`, `src/rules/party.ts#ruined?: Set<string>`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Who besieges and how they travel

1. When an area converts, its own wild youths and adults turn enraged and besiege its new soundsystem. "Own" means they live there (`c.cell`), wherever they stand. Visitors from next door are not enraged. Code: `src/rules/combat/siege.ts#startSiege`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#enrage`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "wouldn't get enraged when the runestone transforms".
2. Never enraged: babies, legends, happy ones, leashed ones, ones dazed, fleeing or wandering home at that moment, and every creature of a friendly area (its legend's quest done). Code: `src/rules/combat/siege.ts#!c.dazed && !c.friendly`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.leashed || c.level === 0 || c.boss || stateOf(c) === "happy") return false`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11 (a friendly area's creatures never besiege; no words quoted).
3. At the conversion the area's wild babies turn happy at once and dance. In a friendly area every wild youth and adult turns happy too, so there is no siege there. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#stepFights`, `src/rules/game.ts#castOn(t) && g.friendly.has(key)`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02: "the wild animals in that area should then become friendly, and you should be able to capture the stump".
4. An enraged creature cannot be invited. Letters stop dead on it. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/affection.ts#blocksLetters`. Ruling: none found (code only).
5. A besieger goes for its soundsystem, unless one of her party (leashed or happy, not its own kind, not a baby) is nearer than the nearest soundsystem it may march on. Then it fights that one first. Code: `src/rules/combat/choose.ts#for (const o of partyList)`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#truce`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04 (same kind never fights same kind; no words quoted).
6. A besieger also goes for her when she is on the ground in its area or inside its attack range. It lets her go once she is out of its area, out of range and 45 m away (`combat.witchLose`). Other wild creatures now follow her anywhere on the ground; only besiegers keep this older rule. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#valid`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#(c.siege && d < attackRange)`, `config/tuning.json#combat.witchLose`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (ordinary pursuers follow her anywhere on the ground until she takes off; no words quoted).
7. Further than 40 m from its target a besieger marches at half its top speed (`combat.marchShare` 0.5), going round lakes. Nearer, it fights at full pace. It marches wherever she is on the map. Code: `src/rules/combat/siege.ts#marchSpeedOf`, `src/rules/combat/moves.ts#d > 40 * S`, `src/rules/game.ts#coarseMarchers`, `config/tuning.json#combat.marchShare`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "Animals seem to walk very slowly when they're going towards soundsystems".
8. When a soundsystem falls, its besiegers march on to the nearest one still standing, but never to one further from the map's centre than they are (10 m of slack). Home's speakers always count. Code: `src/rules/combat/siege.ts#marchOn`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#nearestSound`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#INWARD_SLACK`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "never target a soundsystem further from the centre than they are".
9. At home they go for the nearest speaker still standing, and move to the next as each falls. Code: `src/rules/combat/siege.ts#nearestHomeSpeaker`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08 (home is its ring of speakers, each its own soundsystem; no words quoted here because the health he gave that day is no longer the value).

## How a soundsystem is attacked and lost

1. Only the enraged side damages a soundsystem. Her party, happy creatures and wild creatures that are not enraged never do. Code: `src/rules/combat/flight.ts#if (sh.side === "enraged") for (const [key, h] of s.sounds)`, `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#if (t.kind === "sound") return side === "enraged"`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. Against a soundsystem a besieger uses its old attack from config/combat.json, by its level and whether its kind shoots: a youth nips or spits, an adult mauls or barbs. Its species' own attack, pack moves, charges, swoops and long lunges are not used there. Code: `src/rules/combat/choose.ts#c.fight?.target?.kind === "sound" ? null`, `src/rules/combat/data.ts#attackOf`, `config/combat.json#byLevel.melee`, `config/combat.json#byLevel.ranged`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11 (at a soundsystem its ordinary attack, not one blow a charge; no words quoted).
3. Each blow takes off the level's damage a second (youth 3, adult 10) times the attack's cooldown; an adult's is also times `difficulty.adultStrength` (1.3). So a youth's nip takes 4.5 and an adult's maul takes 26 of the 150. Code: `src/rules/combat/data.ts#damagePerSecond * adultMult(level) * attack.cooldown`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#h.hp = Math.max(0, h.hp - damage)`, `config/combat.json#levels.dps`, `config/combat.json#attacks.nip.cooldown`, `config/combat.json#attacks.maul.cooldown`, `config/tuning.json#difficulty.adultStrength`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "adults are stronger".
4. An angry legend with no witch in its reach throws the first lob or beam of its volley at the nearest standing soundsystem within 420 m, for 150 damage. That is now the whole health of a soundsystem or a home speaker, so one landed lob destroys it. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#Bombarding`, `config/tuning.json#legends.bombard.range`, `config/tuning.json#legends.bombard.damage`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Legend bombards, but prioritises you"; Ed, 2026-11-16: "Keep 150: one lob destroys one".
5. At 0 health an area's soundsystem is lost: the area stops being a party area and is ruined. Its besiegers march on. Nothing else changes. Code: `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#soundDestroyed`, `src/rules/game.ts#g.party.areas.delete(key); S.ruined.add(key)`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "doesn't affect the bpm".
6. Its happy babies run off for the rest of the night, to sleep on the beach. Babies that are leashed, parked, or whose sigil still lies loose on the ground stay hers. Loose sigils stay where they lie. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#runOff(c, runawaySpot(g, c)`, `src/rules/game.ts#!c.leashed && !c.dropped && !c.loose && c.level === 0`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "no creatures are created and none are destroyed".
7. A home speaker at 0 health shows as destroyed and its besiegers move to the nearest standing speaker. Home is lost only with the last speaker. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#if (isHomeKey(key))`, `src/rules/partyOver.ts#homeDown`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-10: "The party should end when all your home speakers are destroyed".

## Defenders

1. Happy creatures of a party area dance at its soundsystem and break off to fight enraged creatures anywhere in their own area. Code: `src/rules/combat/choose.ts#const happy = !c.leashed`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#foes`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#danceAt`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. A creature parked at a placed sigil guards the whole area its sigil is in, plus 80 m round the sigil (its chain's length). Code: `src/rules/combat/reach.ts#guardRadius`, `src/rules/game.ts#areaAt: (x, z) => areaOf`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.length`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "A parked creature should guard its chain length, now that's a fixed number, with home area an exception".
3. Parked in the home area it guards the whole home area and nothing outside it, and roams all of it. Code: `src/rules/combat/choose.ts#atHome ? parkedIn`, `src/rules/leash.ts#roamHome`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "Animals parked in the home area can roam it freely".
4. While she is in the treehouse (on its floor, not knocked out), every creature on her stack, babies too but not a party legend, counts as parked at its own spot in the home area. It goes there and guards home, sieges included. Her stack is not changed, and when she leaves they follow her again. Code: `src/rules/houseGuard.ts#inHouse`, `src/rules/houseGuard.ts#houseGuards`, `src/rules/houseGuard.ts#leashView`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-24: "they should also defend it, as if their sigils are placed in the home area".
5. Creatures following her guard nothing. They only take on what is going for her or for one of hers, and only within 30 m of her (`combat.defendRadius`). A besieger that ignores her is ignored by them. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#acquire`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#const posse = c.leashed && !guarding`, `config/tuning.json#combat.defendRadius`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-09: "Animals that are leashed should attack animals that are attacking you".
6. A party animal that is travelling (far from her or from its sigil), eating a queued berry or evolving fights nobody. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#!(c.leashed && c.travelling)`, `src/rules/combat/standby.ts#(c.leashed && w.busy(c.id))`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "if you have given the instruction to a creature to eat a berry, it should priotise that over fighting".
7. Babies never fight and cannot be hit. Same kind never fights same kind, so a parked wolf does not defend against enraged wolves. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#targetable`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#truce`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04: "No animals should attack babies".

## Winning and losing the night

1. Lost: when the last home speaker falls the party is over, whatever other soundsystems still stand. Losing comes first: once home has fallen the win is no longer checked. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#if (!g.partyOver && homeDown(S)) startPartyOver(g, time)`, `src/rules/win.ts#if (g.partyOver && g.win?.phase !== "won") return`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-10: "The party should end when all your home speakers are destroyed".
2. There is no game-over screen. In the afterparty the waves stop, nothing fights or hurts her, her leash lets go (her stack and her placed sigils are emptied), she is healed, and everyone walks home and sleeps. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#startPartyOver`, `src/rules/partyOver.ts#stepPartyOver`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Animals can all walk home naturally."
3. The win starts when the route's last wave has landed, or sooner when every area has the party or is ruined. Ruined areas count as reached, so a night can be won with soundsystems lost as long as home stands. Code: `src/rules/win.ts#routeDone`, `src/rules/win.ts#islandCleared`, `src/rules/party.ts#allReached`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-12 (the island cleared: the stragglers, then the lap, come at once; no words quoted).
4. Then the stragglers: while any enraged creature that is not a legend is still about, the win waits. Code: `src/rules/win.ts#straggler`, `src/rules/win.ts#nearestStraggler`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11 (enraged legends never hold the win up; no words quoted).
5. With none left, the victory lap's pulses run the line at 120 m/s (`win.lapSpeed`). The night is won when the lap reaches home. Code: `src/rules/win.ts#stepWin`, `src/rules/win.ts#lapRuns`, `config/tuning.json#win.lapSpeed`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-12 (two fast pulses set off at once; no words quoted).
6. At the win her leash is let go, every animal joins the conga line, and only legends whose quest is done dance. Nothing fights or hurts her after. Code: `src/rules/win.ts#function win`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#inConga`, `src/rules/game.ts#worldAsleep(g) || won(g)`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "When conga animals are following me, they shouldn't be leashed to me - they just follow me".
