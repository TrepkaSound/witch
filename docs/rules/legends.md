## Where legends are

1. On an island by roster (the normal game: `map.island` is "home" or a kind) the night's legends are dealt, not picked by share: 1 legend per 5 areas, at least one, each a different kind from the island's roster, never the island's own kind. Code: `src/rules/rosters.ts#legendsFor`, `src/rules/rosters.ts#dealLegends`, `config/tuning.json#map.island`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "Home legends: Same as other islands, which I think is 1 in 5? But including undiscovered islands of phase 1 if possible".
2. `legends.share` (0.2) picks the legend areas only on a map that is not an island by roster: that share of the areas, seeded, spread so no more than two neighbouring legend areas sit together. Code: `src/rules/map.ts#chooseLegendCells`, `config/tuning.json#legends.share`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "only in about half of areas" (a fifth since 2026-10-08).
3. The home area never has a sleeping legend, quest or circle. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#spawnCreatures`, `src/rules/map.ts#chooseLegendCells`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "Home area shouldn't have a legend".
4. On a kind's island (not the home island, not the zoo) one home legend of the island's kind spawns friendly in the home area (`homeGuard`). It has no quest, no rune, takes no letters, is nobody's kin, never walks the route and never leaves home. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#homeGuard`, `src/rules/legends.ts#homeGuard`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#partyEggOpen`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the code).
5. On an island by roster with `legends.areas` on (it is), each kind that could be dealt has an extra legend area grown beside one of its kind's areas (its pair), with no runestone or soundsystem; tonight's legends lie in theirs and the pair counts as one area for kin, rage and fights. Code: `src/rules/map.ts#legendAreas`, `src/rules/map.ts#areaOf`, `config/tuning.json#legends.areas`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "legends are spawned in additional areas that have legend circles where runestones usually are".
6. A legend lies in its clearing, `legendClearing.back` (0.7) of the radius north of the middle, so she can stand in the circle in front of it. That spot is its lair: it comes back there to sleep. Code: `src/rules/legendFit.ts#spotShare`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#lairX`, `config/tuning.json#legendClearing.back`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "Legends should sleep at the back of their circles, so you can stand in the circle before them".
7. Each legend with a clearing starts with one wild baby of its own kind that keeps to the circle, so it starts with kin. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#keepsToCircle`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#pointInCircle`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (quote cut short in the code).

## Asleep, restless, angry

1. A legend has four states: asleep, restless, angry, happy. There is no waking or beaten state. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#LEGEND_STATES`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (no quote in the code).
2. No wave wakes a legend, and neither does a letter or a flask. Only two things change a sleeper: none of its kind in its area (restless, then angry) or its quest done (happy). Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#stepLegendStates`, `src/rules/quest.ts#questPlaced`, `src/rules/inviteWakes.ts#LEGEND`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18 (flasks no longer wake a legend; no quote in the code).
3. Asleep or restless, a legend is scenery: out of every fight, cannot be hit, and letters bounce off it. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#dormant`, `src/rules/affection.ts#blocksLetters`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. Every 5 seconds (each legend on its own beat) it looks for kin in its area. Kin is any creature of its species there in any state, babies too, and her leashed ones only if their sigil is parked in that area. Not kin: one running off, one besieging another area's soundsystem, another legend, the home legend, a follower still on her stack. Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#hasKin`, `src/rules/game.ts#areaOf`, `config/legends.json#check`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "the legends could check for own species in area once every five seconds without issue".
5. With no kin it turns restless at the next check, and after 60 seconds restless it is angry. Kin back while restless puts it straight back to asleep with restlessness 0. Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#stepLegendStates`, `config/legends.json#angryAfter`. Ruling: none found (code only).
6. An angry legend goes back to sleep when kin is back in its area (checked on the same 5 second beat), or when its hearts ring is filled by blows (worn down). Away from its lair it first walks home at 6 m/s, round lakes, then lies down. Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#lull`, `src/rules/legends.ts#walkHome`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#lull`, `config/legends.json#homeSpeed`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Angry legends should go back to sleep once one of their own species is back in their area".
7. When the party is over every legend is put to sleep, and no quest is open. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#legendState`, `src/rules/legends.ts#questsClosed`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18 (no quote in the code).

## Quests and becoming friendly

1. Each sleeping legend dreams of one other species (never its own), chosen from the seed, living in an area later on the route than its own (`legends.questLater` on). There is no distance cap (`legends.questCap` 0). Code: `src/rules/quest.ts#questFor`, `config/tuning.json#legends.questLater`, `config/tuning.json#legends.questCap`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31: "no quest cap".
2. The quest is done only by putting down the sigil of a creature of that species, any age, inside the legend's clearing. Her animal walking in does nothing; a sigil put down outside only flashes the circle. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#questsFromPlaced`, `src/rules/quest.ts#questPlaced`, `src/rules/map.ts#inLegendClearing`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-19: "The reward should be given when the sigil is placed, not when the animal enters the circle".
3. The quest can be done while the legend is asleep or restless, and only while the party is on: not before the party starts, not after it is over, not in the zoo, and never for an angry legend. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#worldAsleep`, `src/rules/quest.ts#questFor_`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-26 (no quote in the code).
4. Quest done: the legend is happy at once and fights for her, and its island is found tonight (kept only if the night is won). It gives no boon and no buff for the quest. Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#cheer`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#questDone`, `src/rules/win.ts#islandsTonight`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18: "Legends accept any level animal for their quest, at which point they fight for you and unlock their island".
5. Done before the area's wave, the area is friendly too: its wild creatures leave her and her party be and count as friends on the stone's stack. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#befriendArea`, `src/rules/quest.ts#done.add`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02: "the wild animals in that area should then become friendly, and you should be able to capture the stump".
6. A quest done does not move the ley route now: `leyLines.advance` is "wave", so only waves do. Code: `src/rules/leylines.ts#questsMove`, `config/tuning.json#leyLines.advance`. Ruling: none found (code only).
7. At the win only legends whose quest is done dance by the dancefloor; every other dealt legend is put back to sleep and its island counts as lost tonight. Code: `src/rules/win.ts#dealtLegends`, `src/rules/win.ts#legendPlaces`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the code).

## Relic flasks

1. A flask is drunk on the spot, never carried: on the ground, standing within 5.625 m of its sigil (which lies 6 m south of the bottle), press the sigil button. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#flaskUnder`, `src/rules/legends.ts#flaskUnder`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runeRadius`, `config/legends.json#relics.sigilOffset`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18: "All flasks give growth spurt (all creatures following you become adults)".
2. Drinking makes every follower on her stack below adult an adult at once (parked ones and legends are not counted). With none to grow, nothing happens but the refusal, and the flask stays. Code: `src/rules/berries.ts#growthSpurt`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#fizzled`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18: "All flasks give growth spurt (all creatures following you become adults)".
3. A flask does nothing to a legend: it does not wake it or make it happy. Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#flaskUnder`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18 (no quote in the code).
4. Flasks are not in the world before the party starts. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#relicsOut`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "Relics, like finds, only appear once the party starts".
5. A map has one flask per 10 areas (at least one, `relics.perAreas`), not one of each kind. The first lies just inside the home area's edge, the rest in areas far from home, at least 150 m apart where the map allows. Code: `src/rules/legends.ts#placeRelics`, `config/legends.json#relics.perAreas`, `config/legends.json#relics.minGap`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11 (no quote in the code).

## Friendly legends: targets and route

1. A friendly (happy) legend cannot be targeted or provoked by anything. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#targetable`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the code).
2. A friendly legend reaches `legends.friendRange` (85 m). First it defends: anything attacking or hunting a witch who is within 85 m of it, even if the attacker itself is further off. Else it takes the enraged, and the wild going for a witch, within 85 m. It leaves calm wild creatures alone. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#attacksWitch`, `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#friendlyEnemy`, `config/tuning.json#legends.friendRange`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (no quote in the code).
3. With nothing to fight, a friendly quest legend walks the whole of tonight's ley route to its far end, drawn that far yet or not. It does not stop at the drawing tip or the pulse. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#friendlyIdle`, `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#walkFriendlyRoute`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "I mean the very end of the route, not where the pulse is".
4. At the far end it turns round and walks the route back to its start at home, then goes to a guard post in the home area and stays there for the rest of the night. Its stage is `journey` (unset: out; "back"; "guard"), saved with the night. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#friendlyIdle`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#journey`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "once it gets to the end of the route, it should turn around and walk back. Once it gets back to the dancefloor, it starts guarding the home area like the home legend".
5. The path is the ley line's own curved links, stone to stone in wave order from home, kept off the water: where the line crosses a lake the legend goes round the shore. It joins the route at the nearest point and walks at `combat.legendRun` (6). Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#friendlyLegendRoute`, `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#dryRoute`, `src/rules/leylines.ts#leyChain`, `config/tuning.json#combat.legendRun`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "Legends walk round lakes".
6. A fight interrupts the walk at any stage, and afterwards it carries on the way it was going. One made friendly near the far end still goes to the end first. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#friendlyIdle`, `src/rules/creatures.ts#heldByCombat`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13 (same ruling as line 4).
7. Guard posts are on rings round the home area's middle: the first 36 m out, 12 m a ring, each post at least 18 m from the home legend's spot and from every other post. These numbers are the builder's, not Ed's. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#GUARD_POSTS`, `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#guardPost`. Ruling: none found (code only).
8. A legend that guards home (the home legend, or a route legend back at its post) cannot leave home: a charging one takes only targets inside home and its charge stops at the edge, and the game puts it back if anything moves it out. Code: `src/rules/creatures.ts#guardsHome`, `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#stepLongCharge`, `src/rules/game.ts#guardsHome`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13 (same ruling as line 4).
9. The home legend never walks the route and skips the defend-her-first step; it only takes enemies within its own 85 m. A returned route legend still defends her first. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#stepLegendAttack`. Ruling: none found (code only).
10. A friendly charging legend does not walk back to its lair after a charge: it rests 2 seconds and carries on. Only an angry one walks back (at 6 m/s). Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#stepLongCharge`, `config/legends.json#charge.rest`, `config/legends.json#charge.returnSpeed`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Legend attacks and damage

1. An angry legend stands at its lair and reaches 420 m. It shoots witches on the ground and her leashed creatures, never happy creatures, up to 3 targets at once, every 15 seconds after a 2.2 second windup. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#stepLegendAttack`, `config/legends.json#attack.range`, `config/legends.json#attack.targets`, `config/legends.json#attack.interval`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (no quote in the code).
2. At her and at creatures it casts its own kind's attack made big: a beam kind one giant beam across its reach, every other kind a huge slow lob (3 s flight, 8 m radius) that bursts into its kind's attack where it lands. A friendly legend's lobs home onto their target. Code: `src/rules/attacks/species.ts#legendOf`, `src/rules/attacks/species.ts#legendAttack`, `config/legends.json#attack.lobFlight`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-27: "an exaggerated version of their species' attack: bigger, slower, longer range, more children".
3. The damage handed to the cast is plain `attack.damage` (10). It is not multiplied by a level's power or by the interval. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#A.damage`, `config/legends.json#attack.damage`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. Charging kinds (boar, elk, stag, ram, ibex, hedgehog, woodlouse, rhino, unicorn) charge instead: 2.5 s head down, then a long run trampling everything in a 6 m lane for 10 damage each, once each. An angry one throws her aside; a friendly one passes her and her side. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#stepLongCharge`, `config/legends.json#charge.species`, `config/legends.json#charge.laneWidth`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (no quote in the code).
5. An angry legend with no witch in reach bombards the nearest standing soundsystem within 420 m with the old lob or beam, for `legends.bombard.damage` (150); a soundsystem's health is 150. Code: `src/rules/combat/legend.ts#bombard`, `config/tuning.json#legends.bombard.damage`, `config/tuning.json#combat.soundsystemHealth`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Legend bombards, but prioritises you".

## The legend circle and the challenge circle

1. On the ground inside the circle of a legend that is asleep or restless, nothing from outside can go for her or land on her (shelter). An angry or happy legend's circle gives no shelter, and the treetops give none through this rule. Code: `src/rules/slowTime.ts#calmRingAt`, `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#sheltered`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#sheltered`. Ruling: Ed, round 14 playtest (no date in the code): "I got attacked by a wild creature when in a legend circle; I think they shouldn't attack you from outside when you're in there".
2. In that same calm circle the world runs at 0.1 speed while she moves at full speed, enraged creatures are pushed out to its edge, and a letter that leaves the circle vanishes. Code: `src/rules/slowTime.ts#slowTarget`, `src/rules/slowTime.ts#keepEnragedOut`, `src/rules/slowTime.ts#leavesCalmRing`, `config/tuning.json#legendCircle.slow.scale`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "freeze only if the legend is asleep or restless. Enraged creatures outside the circle don't enter it. Your invitations disappear if they go outside the circle from inside".
3. The panel beside the circle shows for a legend in any state, on the ground only, and offers the quest only while the quest is open. Code: `src/rules/legendCircle.ts#circleLines`, `src/rules/legendCircle.ts#legendCircleNear`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "when you go into a legend circle, text appears on the screen to the side of the circle explaining mechanics to do with legends".
4. The challenge circle is not a legend circle: it has no legend, no quest, no shelter and no slow time. Its pack is one adult and one youth of every kind on the island, stone statues until she sets foot on its floor. Code: `src/rules/challenge.ts#packKinds`, `src/rules/challengeStatues.ts#statue`, `config/tuning.json#challenge.adults`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "one adult and one youth of every creature kind on the island".

## Inviting a legend and legends on her leash

1. No legend can be invited, chatted to or leashed in the normal way, and an ordinary friendly legend has no rune to pick up. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/leash.ts#inviteCreature`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#hasRune`. Ruling: COORDINATOR #1552, 2026-10-31 (no Ed quote in the code).
2. The one exception is the optional party egg (`legends.partyEgg`, on): a friendly quest legend that is free takes letters, and its 100th (`legends.partyHits`) makes it a party legend. Its meter drains 1% a second. Sleeping, restless and angry legends, and the home legend, still refuse letters. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#partyEggOpen`, `src/rules/game.ts#meterHits`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyHits`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyDrain`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Maybe you should be able to turn a happy legend into a party legend with an absurd number of invites (100?) and then pick up its sigil but it's totally useless".
3. A party legend dances where it stands, fights no one, leaves the route, and its rune can be picked up. Leashed, it never comes to her and she cannot go more than 6.8 m from it, on the ground or in the air; putting its sigil down frees her. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#makePartyLegend`, `src/rules/partyLegend.ts#pinWitch`, `src/rules/leash.ts#letPartyLegendGo`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyReach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "It would be funny if you could leash an elder, but it's so heavy that it stops you moving at all outside the leash slack distance, and it doesn't move towards you at all."
4. A legend on her stack (in play, only a party legend) stops her taking off from the ground, as an adult does. Youths, babies and parked animals never do, and once she is up it does not bring her down. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#holdsDown`, `src/rules/leashWeight.ts#groundedBy`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "I think leashed youths shouldn't stop you from taking off, only adults and legends do."
5. When a stone converts, its legend's sigil falls loose with the friends' sigils unless the legend is angry. Picking it up does not leash the legend: it tethers her to the sleeping legend at the distance the sigil lay from it, or 80 m if that is more, until she puts it down anywhere. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#friendSigils`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#tetherWitch`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#chainLength`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.length`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "too heavy to drag ... she can't go further than one leash length from the sleeping legend".
