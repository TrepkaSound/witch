## Speeds on the ground and in the treetops

1. On the ground she moves at 19.25 m/s and reaches the stick's speed almost at once (ground acceleration 28). Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#stepWitch`, `config/tuning.json#groundSpeed`, `config/tuning.json#groundAcceleration`. Ruling: none found (code only). The comment quotes Ed without a date: "ground should feel snappy".
2. In the treetops she cruises at 48 m/s with momentum. Holding a direction within 25 degrees for 2 s builds a boost up to 1.7 times cruise (81.6 m/s); a turn of 90 degrees or more bleeds it 3 times a second; letting go, she glides to a stop over about 1 s. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#treetopFlight`, `config/tuning.json#treetopSpeed`, `config/tuning.json#treetop.boost`, `config/tuning.json#treetop.boostTime`, `config/tuning.json#treetop.boostAngle`, `config/tuning.json#treetop.sharpTurnBleed`, `config/tuning.json#treetop.glideTime`. Ruling: none found (code only). The comment quotes Ed without a date: "a high top speed and more momentum".
3. Her broom's parts change these numbers for her alone (speeds, rise and fall, cooldowns, blink distance and count). Every number on this page is the bare broom's. Code: `src/rules/broomParts.ts#broomTuning`, `config/broom-parts.json#parts`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-23 (the Workshop; no quote in the code).
4. Creatures only aim blows at her while she is on foot: mode "ground", not seated, not on a treehouse floor. Rising, in the treetops or coming down, nothing targets her. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#onGround`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#v.onGround`, `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#v.onGround`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Rising and coming down

1. One button. Rising takes 0.7 s and coming down 0.55 s (height 1.4 m to 24 m); a press part way reverses it. Her mode leaves "ground" the moment she starts to rise, so creatures lose her at once. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#toggleMode`, `config/tuning.json#riseTime`, `config/tuning.json#descendTime`, `config/tuning.json#groundHeight`, `config/tuning.json#treetopHeight`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "players rise to the treetops instead of dodging".
2. Blink, take-off and landing share one cooldown counter with 2 s of room. A blink adds 1 s, a take-off 2 s, a landing 3 s. So after landing: no rising for 3 s and no blink for 2 s; after one blink, no take-off for 1 s. Code: `src/rules/cooldown.ts#coolReady`, `src/rules/cooldown.ts#coolSpend`, `src/rules/game.ts#coolReady`, `config/tuning.json#cooldown.shared`, `config/tuning.json#cooldown.rise`, `config/tuning.json#riseCooldown`, `config/tuning.json#dash.cooldown`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-15: "One trail, one cooldown. ... Its length shows one shared cooldown counter for dodge and take-off (the two contribute different amounts to it)".
3. She cannot press to come down over a lake (the water plus 1.5 m): the press is dropped and she flies on. Already coming down and drifting over one, she hovers at 0.3 of treetop lift until she is over the shore. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#lakeHold`, `src/rules/witch.ts#LAKE_HOVER`, `config/tuning.json#lakes.pad`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-23: "At the moment you can press x to leave treetop mode while over a lake; you go lower and move more slowly. I don't think you should be able to do that".

## What grounds her or pulls her down

1. With an adult or a legend in her stack (a party legend too) she cannot take off from the ground. Youths, babies and parked creatures never stop her. Code: `src/rules/leashWeight.ts#groundedBy`, `src/rules/chains.ts#holdsDown`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "I think leashed youths shouldn't stop you from taking off, only adults and legends do".
2. The refused take-off spends no cooldown and is no dodge: she never leaves ground mode. It only stops a take-off from the ground (never in the zoo); one who grows up or joins while she is in the air does not bring her down by this rule. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#W.body.mode === "ground" && !W.body.seated && !g.map.bounds.fence && groundedBy`, `src/rules/game.ts#kind: "grounded"`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "I think leashed youths shouldn't stop you from taking off, only adults and legends do".
3. Every chained follower is on an 80 m chain. Past its end, moving away, her motion is cut by the heaviest taut chain only: in the air youth 0.3, adult 0.7, legend 1 (stopped); on foot 0.15, 0.4, 1. No chains while she is knocked out, on a treehouse floor or in the zoo. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#dragWitch`, `src/rules/chains.ts#chainDrag`, `src/rules/game.ts#!g.map.bounds.fence && !W.ko && !W.body.perch`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.length`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.drag`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.groundDrag`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "pulling on the ground should slow you down less than pulling in the air".
4. In the treetops, pulling past a taut chain tugs her down gradually: 0.05 of the way per metre of pull, rising back at 0.3 a second when she stops. Pulled all the way she lands (the landing cooldown applies), but never on a lake or a treehouse floor: there she is held at 0.4 of the way. A throw is not her pulling. Code: `src/rules/tautChain.ts#stepTug`, `src/rules/game.ts#const hold`, `src/rules/game.ts#free: thrown`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.tug.perMetre`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.tug.rise`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.tug.hold`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "Treetop pulldown is somewhat gradual - you have to tug for a while before you're pulled down."
5. Pulling on past a legend's chain, she is yanked 4 m back toward it, at most once every 1.2 s, with no stagger. Code: `src/rules/tautChain.ts#yankFrom`, `src/rules/game.ts#kind: "yanked"`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.yank.metres`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.yank.every`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "legend chains yank you back instead of popping out".
6. A party legend in her stack pins her within 6.8 m of it, and a sleeping legend's sigil she carries tethers her, however she moves (walking, blinking, flying or thrown). Code: `src/rules/game.ts#pinWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#tetherWitch`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyReach`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## The blink

1. Ground only. No blink while seated, on a treehouse floor, staggered or knocked out. A press up to 0.2 s early is held and blinks as soon as it can. Code: `src/rules/dash.ts#startDash`, `src/rules/game.ts#!stunned(W.knock, ht)`, `src/rules/game.ts#bufferUntil`, `config/tuning.json#dash.buffer`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. She moves 15 m in one step, the way she is steering; standing still, the way she faces. Blinking toward the cursor is off. Code: `src/rules/dash.ts#startDash`, `config/tuning.json#dash.distance`, `config/tuning.json#dash.toCursor`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "the witch's dodge should be more like 10 metres, and be a 'blink' - she vanishes and reappears in the direction of travel". The live 15 m is recorded in DESIGN.md as Ed's +50% of 2026-10-11, with no quote.
3. She has 2 blinks (the Twig bristles and the Climbers buff add more). Two go straight off, at least 0.2 s apart; the third waits 1 s. With the shared cooldown on, the counter decides, not the charges kept in the dash state. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#blinkCharges`, `src/rules/dash.ts#gate`, `config/tuning.json#dash.charges`, `config/tuning.json#dash.chain`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08 (two blinks before the cooldown; no quote in the code).
4. She is gone, and cannot be hit, for 0.2 s, not 0.05 s: with committed strikes on, the longer of `dash.gone` and `dodge.a.gone` is used. Code: `src/rules/dash.ts#blinkGone`, `config/tuning.json#dash.gone`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.gone`, `config/tuning.json#dodge.a.on`. Ruling: none found (code only).
5. While she is gone, a hit, a throw, a pack's pull, a creature's stun (now a slow) and a force nudge all do nothing. A plain slow is not checked against the blink in this code. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#hitWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#throwWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#stunWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#nudgeWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#slowWitch`. Ruling: none found (code only).
6. A blink lands wherever it takes her: through and onto trees, rocks, soundsystems, speakers and the treehouse's tree. Only two things shorten it: landing on a lake (the water plus 1.5 m) on foot, and crossing the zoo's fence. It is then cut back in half-metre steps to the furthest allowed spot, and always kept on the island. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#blinkClear`, `src/rules/dash.ts#r -= 0.5`, `src/rules/dash.ts#keepIn`, `config/tuning.json#lakes.pad`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "There's nothing to collide with apart from lakes".
7. Only the landing spot is tested for water, so a blink can carry her across a strip of lake narrower than the blink. Code: `src/rules/dash.ts#if (clear(x, z))`, `src/rules/game.ts#onLake(g.map.bounds, x, z, lakeKnobs(g.tuning).pad)`. Ruling: none found (code only).
8. The `dash.clear` clearances (tree 1.2, rock 2.5, soundsystem 3, speaker 1.5, treehouse 4 m) no longer apply to her; only the party witches round the floor use them. The Ley jump broom spell lands by the same test as a blink. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#clearOfScenery`, `src/rules/game.ts#leyJump(g, W, (x, z) => blinkClear(g, x, z, W.body))`, `config/tuning.json#dash.clear`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Drop all of them".

## Being thrown

1. A blow that costs her a hit, but does not knock her out, throws her straight away from the attacker (for a shot or lob, from where it landed): 2.5 m, plus 0.55 times the attack's knockback, at least 12 m if it rams her (a charge, a leap, a legend's long run), at most 14 m. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#hitWitch`, `src/rules/knock.ts#knockOf`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#landUnobserved`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.base`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.scale`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.charge`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.max`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "add a knockback and stun on the witch; make it large on chasing/ramming creatures".
2. The badger's barge (its own lunge's melee blow only) throws her 20 m, past the 14 m cap, and staggers her 0.5 s. Its bite and its pack's stone shard throw her as any blow does. PR #2072. Code: `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#barge`, `src/rules/knock.ts#far`, `config/tuning.json#badgerBarge.throw`, `config/tuning.json#badgerBarge.stagger`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "20m".
3. A pack's ram throws her an exact distance, and a pack's pull throws her toward the puller (never closer than 1.5 m, and not at all from under 2 m). An attack's knockback effect throws her without costing a hit. All three are held to the 14 m cap. Code: `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#packThrow`, `src/rules/game.ts#pull: (id, x, z, metres)`, `src/rules/game.ts#throwWitch`, `src/rules/knock.ts#Math.min(K.max, metres)`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. A throw starts at 10 times its distance in m/s and eases off, so most of it is over in a quarter of a second. It goes straight through scenery. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#stepWitchKnock`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.ease`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "don't make it stop at scenery".
5. During a throw she is kept on the island every step, and on foot kept 1.5 m ashore of every lake: thrown at a lake, she stops at its shore. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#ashore(bounds, q.x, q.z, pad)`, `src/rules/knock.ts#keepIn`, `src/rules/lakes.ts#ashore`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "There's nothing to collide with apart from lakes".
6. When the throw ends she stays where it left her. Only if that spot is a lake on foot, or over the zoo's fence, is she moved to the nearest allowed spot (searched out to 8 m). No nudging clear of trees, rocks, soundsystems, speakers or the treehouse. PR #2074. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#nearestClear`, `src/rules/game.ts#stepWitchKnock(W.knock, W.body, hdt, t, g.map.bounds, (x, z) => blinkClear(g, x, z, W.body))`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "Drop all of them".
7. A blow also staggers her: 0.25 s, plus 0.07 s per metre thrown past 2.5 m, at most 1 s. Staggered, she cannot move, rise, blink or throw invitations; the sigil buttons still work. For 1 s after a stagger she cannot be staggered again, but blows still throw her and cost hits. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#immuneUntil`, `src/rules/game.ts#stunned(W.knock, ht)`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.stunBase`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.stunScale`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.stunMax`, `config/tuning.json#witch.knock.immune`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05: "add a knockback and stun on the witch; make it large on chasing/ramming creatures".
8. The blow that knocks her out throws nothing. A blow inside the 0.5 s grace after a hit does nothing at all, so no throw. A blow the Helmet takes still throws her. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#hitWitch`, `config/tuning.json#witchHealth.grace`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31 (grace 0.5 confirmed; no quote in the code).
9. An attack's push or pull field moves her directly, a little each step, on foot only. That function has no lake or edge test of its own. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#nudgeWitch`, `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#force(t, fx, fz, push, dt, source)`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Collisions: what stops her and what does not

1. Lakes are the only scenery she collides with. On foot she stops 1.5 m from the water and slides along the shore as she steers. Rising, in the treetops or coming down she passes over them: the shore stop runs only in ground mode. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#stopAtShore`, `src/rules/witch.ts#mode === "ground" && bounds.lakes`, `src/rules/lakes.ts#stopAtShore`, `config/tuning.json#lakes.pad`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "There's nothing to collide with apart from lakes".
2. Trees, rocks, ruins, wall objects, rune stones, soundsystems, speakers and the treehouse's tree do not stop or slow her, walking, blinking or thrown. Her step has no test for any of them. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#stepWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#blinkClear`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "There's nothing to collide with apart from lakes".
3. Creatures do not block or push her (the body spacing step moves creatures only), and area borders do not stop her (nothing in her step reads which area she is in). Code: `src/rules/game.ts#stepSpacing`, `src/rules/witch.ts#stepWitch`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. The island's edge holds her softly: within 80 m of the coast her outward speed is cut, down to nothing at the coast, with a drift of up to 6 m/s back in over the outer half. She can never be past the coast. Code: `src/rules/mapShape.ts#softEdge`, `src/rules/mapShape.ts#keepIn`, `config/tuning.json#map.push`, `config/tuning.json#map.drift`. Ruling: none found (code only).
5. Pushing on out to sea on the sand for 0.8 s brings her down (from the treetops too) and she lies stargazing. A rise or any other direction gets her up. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#stargazing`, `src/rules/witch.ts#seaward`, `config/tuning.json#beach.restAfter`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "If you try and fly past the beach, you land and stargaze".
6. In the zoo only, the gold fences stop her on foot; she flies over them. Pushing against the zoo's edge for 0.5 s leaves the zoo. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#bounds.fence.stop`, `src/rules/game.ts#ZOO_EDGE_PUSH`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "the witch can fly over the fence, not walk".

## Slows

1. A creature's stun on her is now a slow: 40% speed for 3 times the stun's length. PR #2073. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#stunWitch`, `config/tuning.json#witch.stunAsSlow.on`, `config/tuning.json#witch.stunAsSlow.speed`, `config/tuning.json#witch.stunAsSlow.time`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-13: "I think stun and slow are very similar, and stun is less fun than slow, so how about stun is replaced with slow and we use circling stars?".
2. A slow multiplies her top speed on the ground and in the treetops alike. Slows do not multiply together: the end time is the latest, and the strength is the stronger of the new one and any older one that lasts longer. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#slowMult`, `src/rules/game.ts#slowUntil`. Ruling: none found (code only).
3. A root on her is a slow to 0.05 of her speed, not a full stop. Code: `src/rules/attacks/combatWorld.ts#w.slowWitch?.(id, time + e.root, 0.05)`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. Every blow's own short stagger (see Being thrown) was not changed: it is still a full stop of 0.25 to 1 s. Code: `src/rules/knock.ts#stunUntil`, `src/rules/game.ts#stunned(W.knock, ht)`. Ruling: none found (code only). Ed's 2026-11-13 stun ruling does not mention the stagger.

## Knockout and respawn

1. The fourth hit knocks her out where she stands: no input, and no throw. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#knockOut`, `src/rules/knockout.ts#hurt`, `config/tuning.json#witchHealth.hits`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "three slashes, and then a final hit".
2. Half way through the 1.2 s teleport she is moved to the treehouse: with the house on, to the magic circle, where she rises for 0.9 s and walks to her decks. She ends seated, on the treehouse floor, until her first move or rise. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#stepKnockout`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.teleport`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.rise`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.house.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "When you die and respawn, you should appear behind the decks in your treehouse, as when the game begins (but not zoomed in)".
3. She can move again 6 s after going down, flat (no extra for repeat knockdowns: step 0), moved to the nearest bar line and never under 1.5 s of scratching. The desk's candles count the wait, one a second. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#respawnWait`, `src/rules/knockout.ts#candleCount`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.respawn.base`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.respawn.step`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.respawn.minScratch`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.candleStep`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "any more than about six seconds to wait will be frustrating. We could alternatively make each successive death a bit longer, with a cooldown, to punish rapid dying".

## The treehouse floor (the perch)

1. Flying in at treetop height over any room or branch floor, from outside it, lands her there with no button; pressing to come down over a floor lands her on it too. Landing takes 0.35 s and eases her onto clear floor, never into furniture or a station. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#flyOnto`, `src/rules/treehouse.ts#landsOn`, `src/rules/treehouse.ts#landingSpot`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.house.landTime`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-14 (the house's balcony; no quote in the code).
2. For 3 s after leaving a floor she cannot land on one, and for 3 s after landing she cannot rise or step off. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#perchCool`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.landCooldown`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "Taking off from the treehouse and landing on the treehouse; there should be a minimum time between which either these things can happen, perhaps 3 seconds, because it's easy to e.g. take off from a branch and land immediately on the next branch".
3. On the floor she walks at 9 m/s, cannot blink, has no chain drag and is out of every fight. Code: `src/rules/witch.ts#deck.house.walk`, `src/rules/dash.ts#w.perch`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.house.walk`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-21: "she shouldn't use her broom until she steps off the balcony".
4. With her broom she can rise from any room or branch. Without one she cannot rise indoors. Code: `src/rules/treehouse.ts#noRiseAt`, `src/rules/game.ts#hasBroom(W)`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "maybe you can take off from anywhere on the treehouse".
5. Walking off a floor's edge with her broom, she flies (rising). With no broom she falls and is back at the magic circle 1.5 s later (0.9 s fall, 0.6 s beat). With `noWalls` on, every floor edge is open this way, not only the Terrace's take-off. Code: `src/rules/treehouse.ts#keepInHouse`, `src/rules/witch.ts#falling`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.house.look.noWalls`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.house.fall.time`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.house.fall.beat`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## Sailing between islands

1. She never flies or walks to another island: the coast holds her (see Collisions). She sails by choosing an island on the sea chart at the map table, which starts a new night there on the same page; the camera flies in from the south, and her position is not carried over. Code: `src/rules/treehouse.ts#atMapTable`, `src/app/pageNight.ts#sailFromChart`, `src/app/sail.ts#sailWay`, `src/rules/arrive.ts#Handoff`, `config/tuning.json#sail.inPlace`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-12: "fly from the south end of the island, over the sea and over the forest, to give a taste of the terrain, before settling on the home area".
