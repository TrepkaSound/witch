# Rules now: the leash, chains and sigils

<!-- summary: How a creature becomes hers, the sigil stack, placing and picking up, the cast and the stone stacks, chains and what grounds her, babies, losing followers and flasks, as the code does it now. -->

Checked against the code on 2026-11-15. If code and this page disagree, the code is what the game does: fix the page, and tell the coordinator if the code looks wrong.

## Making a creature hers

1. It takes two steps. Her letters fill a wild youth's or adult's meter (7 hits a youth, 14 an adult) and make it happy. It is hers (leashed) only when she picks up its sigil with E on the ground. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#affectionOf`, `src/rules/game.ts#meterHits`, `src/rules/leash.ts#inviteCreature`, `config/states.json#leash`, `config/tuning.json#invites.hits`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (a happy one's sigil rune, picked up to leash it; no words quoted).
2. In an area whose stone still stands, a creature made happy has no rune at its feet. Its sigil turns friend-coloured in the stone's stack and can be picked up only after the stone converts. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#markAtStone`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.atStone || c.loose) return false`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stoneOpen`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "It's a spell that you're choosing to cast on this stone, rather than a property of the area/stone itself."
3. In an area that already has its soundsystem, or is ruined, the happy creature carries its rune at its feet. E within 5.625 m of the rune picks it up (`leash.runeRadius`), and from 8 m away the creature trots over to her. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#hasRune`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#runeNear`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#pullRune`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runeRadius`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runePull.radius`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-12: "Pull from any side".
4. No baby can be invited: no meter, no rune, no chat. A baby joins her only through its loose sigil after its area converts. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/game.ts#if (c.level === 0) return;`, `src/rules/leash.ts#Soundsystem loose-sigil pickup only`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31: "No, no baby is invitable".
5. A legend is never leashed. The one exception is a party legend (a happy legend given 100 letters; the island's home legend and a route legend back home too, since Ed, 2026-11-15): she cannot go more than 6.8 m from it, it never moves, and putting its sigil down lets it go. A home legend let go goes back to guarding home. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#Only the completed partyEgg may make a legend available for pickup`, `src/rules/partyLegend.ts#pinWitch`, `src/rules/leash.ts#letPartyLegendGo`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyHits`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyReach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Maybe you should be able to turn a happy legend into a party legend with an absurd number of invites (100?) and then pick up its sigil but it's totally useless".
6. Talking (the old chat invite) runs only when her letters are off. `invites.on` is true, so the talk times and ranges do nothing in play. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#talk: !t.invites.on`, `config/tuning.json#invites.on`. Ruling: none found (code only).

## The stack and its order

1. The stack lists her creatures oldest first. The newest is the bottom, nearest her hat, and the bottom is what E puts down, and what the command button (F, the pad's square) commands or feeds; with `?commands=0` E feeds it. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#stackBottom`, `src/rules/leash.ts#s.stack.push(c.id)`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. Q moves the bottom sigil to the top, on the ground or in the treetops. The pad's L1 moves the top to the bottom. The one-button controls (the pad's use button and touch's sigil button) cycle in the treetops and place on the ground. The pad's use button is circle; with `?commands=0` it is square, as before 2026-11-17. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#cycleStack`, `src/rules/leash.ts#cycleStackBack`, `src/rules/leash.ts#(c.sigil && !onGround) || c.cycle`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "E for place, Q for cycle".
3. Sigils go down and come up only on the ground: never from the treetops, and never in the treehouse. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#const pressed = (c.sigil || c.place) && onGround`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#const perched`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. A follower on her stack roams within 8 m of her (`leash.length`). More than about 60 m from her on the ground, or whenever she is in the treetops, it travels instead: quiet, fighting nobody, until it is back near her. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#stepLeashed`, `src/rules/travel.ts#updateMode`, `config/tuning.json#leash.length`, `config/travel.json#posse`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (travelling and posse; no words quoted).

## Placing and picking up sigils

1. One press of E does one thing. Checked in this order: a mushroom or basket, her hat lying near, tonight's find, the cast at a stone, a flask under her, feeding (only with `?commands=0`: with commands on, the default since 2026-11-17, E never feeds), then the leash's own press. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#stepSigilButton`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#feedOffer`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. The leash's own press, in order: pick up her placed sigil in the hex cell she stands in; else a loose sigil within 5.625 m; else a happy creature's rune within 5.625 m; else put the bottom sigil down. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#pressTargets`, `src/rules/leash.ts#sigilPress`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runeRadius`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (her own placed sigil first, then the nearest rune; no words quoted).
3. Placed sigils sit on a world-fixed hex grid 6 m across, one a cell. A cell under a rune stone or soundsystem (3 m) or a home speaker (1.5 m) is barred: the sigil then goes to the nearest free cell within two cells, or nothing happens. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#hexSpot`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#sigilBarred`, `config/tuning.json#leash.hex.size`, `config/tuning.json#leash.hex.stone`, `config/tuning.json#leash.hex.speaker`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "a cluster of placed sigils reads cleanly".
4. A parked creature rests just north of its sigil, by its size, and roams within 8 m of it. In the home area it roams the whole area. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#runeOffset(c, t, 1)`, `src/rules/leash.ts#roamHome`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "Animals parked in the home area can roam it freely".
5. Holding E after a press that picked up or put down starts a run only after 0.35 s (`leash.runAfter`). Then she keeps picking up every sigil she walks over, or puts the next one down in each empty hex she enters. A tap does exactly one thing. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#runAfter`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runAfter`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runs`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "make holds feel more deliberate".
6. If the bottom of her stack can eat and a free berry lies within 15 m, a tap of the command button (F, the mouse's middle button, the pad's square, touch's cmd) feeds it instead of commanding; E only places. With `?commands=0`, as before 2026-11-17, a tap of E feeds it instead of placing, and holding 0.3 s puts the sigil down. No animal eats on its own. Code: `src/rules/commands/command.ts`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#feedOffer`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#feedBottom`, `config/tuning.json#berries.feed.reach`, `config/tuning.json#berries.feed.hold`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "something she does deliberately and accurately under time pressure"; Ed, 2026-11-17: "square as command, and circle as pickup/place. When you're near a berry, square becomes eat."; "Put the switches on by default and then publish".
7. Holding E for 0.5 s with nothing to do puts her hat down (`leash.hatHold`). Not on a stump and not at her decks. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#putHat(W.hat, idle`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#const useTarget`, `config/tuning.json#leash.hatHold`. Ruling: none found (code only).
8. A sigil put down in a sleeping legend's clearing finishes its quest. Only the drop counts; the animal walking in does nothing. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#questsFromPlaced`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-19: "The reward should be given when the sigil is placed, not when the animal enters the circle".

## The cast and stone stacks

1. `cast.on` is true. Every stone not yet converted carries a stack of its own creatures' sigils: enemy fighters at the bottom, friends above, babies on top. While any hostile youth or adult is left, the column shows only the hostile ones. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stoneStack`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#hostileSigil`, `config/tuning.json#cast.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "you mostly care about how many creatures are still hostile"; Ed, 2026-11-16: "Keep the code" (only the hostile ones shown while any are left).
2. She can cast only when no wild youth or adult of that area is still hostile: each is happy, leashed, friendly (its legend's quest done) or has run off. Babies and the sleeping legend do not count. A wave converts the stone whatever is left. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#castReady`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stackFriend`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "Stop you from casting until all wild animals are invited".
3. To cast she stands on foot on the stump's cut (not its roots) and holds E for 4 s (`cast.time`). While she is there E does nothing else. With the stumps on, `cast.spot` and `cast.radius` are not used. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#pastCasting`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stepCast`, `src/rules/stumps.ts#stumpCutOf`, `config/tuning.json#cast.time`, `config/tuning.json#runeMarkers.stump.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "The area in which I can cast the soundsystem spell should be the whole trunk. (cut part, not the roots)".
4. Letting go or stepping off stops the cast, and her progress drains at 0.15 of a full cast a second. A hit or a thrown blow breaks it, and she must let go and hold again. Each witch has her own progress on each stone. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#castBroken`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#castProgress`, `config/tuning.json#cast.drain`. Ruling: none found (code only).
5. A conversion is the same by cast or by wave. The soundsystem rises, the area's babies turn happy, any wild youths and adults left besiege it, and every friend sigil in the stack (happy natives, babies, the legend unless angry) falls loose on the ground 4 to 20 m round the stone, never in the hidden wedge north of it. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#scatterFriends`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#friendSigils`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#scatterSpots`, `config/tuning.json#cast.scatter.min`, `config/tuning.json#cast.scatter.reach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "It's fine if they're scattered around the whole runestone clearing".
6. A loose sigil is anyone's. E within 5.625 m picks it up and leashes its creature, a baby too. It stays on the ground even if the soundsystem later falls. A legend circle's baby's sigil lands in its circle, not by the soundsystem: at the circle's left or right edge, so the middle and front, where she puts a quest sigil down, are out of its pick-up reach (in every circle of 7.1 m or more; the smallest is 9). Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#takeLoose`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#looseNear`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#circleSpot`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "this baby's sigil should land in the legend circle instead of near the soundsystem"; Ed, 2026-11-16: "It is easy to go into a legend's circle and accidentally pick up the legend baby instead of putting down the quest sigil ... Perhaps we can move it to the left or right edge".
7. A sleeping legend's loose sigil does not leash it. She carries it off the stack, one at a time, and cannot go further from the legend than 80 m or the distance the sigil lay from it, whichever is more. Her next put-down drops it loose again. If she is knocked out it falls loose at the legend's feet. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#tether: Math.max(chainLength(g.tuning)`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#tetherWitch`, `src/rules/game.ts#if (w.leash.tether)`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "too heavy to drag ... she can't go further than one leash length from the sleeping legend".

## Chains and what grounds her

1. The weight system is gone. Carried sigils do not slow her, drift her, slow her rise or pull her down, and the load code is gone. Code: `src/rules/leashWeight.ts#groundedBy`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "the weight system goes".
2. She cannot take off from the ground while an adult or a legend (a party legend too) is in her stack. Youths, babies and parked creatures never ground her, and species strength does not matter. Code: `src/rules/leashWeight.ts#groundedBy`, `src/rules/chains.ts#holdsDown`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "I think leashed youths shouldn't stop you from taking off, only adults and legends do".
3. The refused take-off spends no cooldown and raises the "grounded" event. If she is already up when a youth grows up, she stays up; it only stops her next take-off. Nothing grounds her in the zoo, nor at the treehouse (its floors, rooms and balcony: wherever her body is `perch`ed): adults and legends in her stack never stop her taking off there. `?treehousetakeoff=old` (`treehouse.freeTakeOff` off) grounds her there too, as before. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#kind: "grounded"`, `src/rules/game.ts#!g.map.bounds.fence && groundedBy`, `src/rules/leashWeight.ts#at?.perch`, `config/tuning.json#treehouse.freeTakeOff`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26 (in the zoo flying is the only way over its fences; no words quoted); Ed, 2026-11-16: "Let her take off from the treehouse".
4. Every youth, adult and legend in her stack is on a chain 80 m long (`leash.chain.length`). Babies and a party legend have none. Inside 80 m a chain does nothing. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#chained`, `src/rules/chains.ts#chainLength`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.length`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "about the size of a fight (80m?)".
5. At a chain's end, moving away, only the heaviest taut chain counts; they do not add up. In the air it takes 0.3 (youth), 0.7 (adult) or all (legend) of her motion away. On foot it takes 0.15, 0.4 or all. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#chainDrag`, `src/rules/chains.ts#dragWitch`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.drag.adult`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.groundDrag.adult`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "pulling on the ground should slow you down less than pulling in the air".
6. In the treetops, pulling past a chain's end tugs her down little by little. If she stops pulling she rises again; pulled all the way she lands. The tug never lands her over a lake or onto a treehouse floor. Code: `src/rules/tautChain.ts#stepTug`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.tug.perMetre`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.tug.hold`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "When you are in treetop mode and you are pulling a creature on its chain, when the chain gets taut you should be pulled back to ground mode."
7. On foot, pulling steadily on a youth's or adult's taut chain for 2.5 s slides its sigil out of her stack and puts it down where she stands. Easing off drains the meter in 1.5 s. Code: `src/rules/tautChain.ts#stepPop`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#popSigil`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.pop.fill`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.pop.drain`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "only if you keep pulling it plops onto the ground as if placed".
8. A legend's sigil never pops. Pulling past its chain yanks her 4 m back toward it, at most once every 1.2 s. Code: `src/rules/tautChain.ts#yankFrom`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.yank.metres`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.yank.every`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "legend chains yank you back instead of popping out".
9. A creature whose chain is taut hurries after her at 1.5 times its leash pace. Chains do nothing while she is knocked out, in the treehouse or in the zoo. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#hurryOf`, `src/rules/gameSteps.ts#chainLength(t) > 0 && !g.map.bounds.fence && !W.ko && !W.body.perch`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.taut.hurry`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "so pulling the army along on the ground feels like leading them".

## Leash ranks

1. `leash.ranks.on` is true. Every leash sigil is drawn in its rank's colour, not its species'. Hers are warm: baby pink, youth crimson, adult orange, legend gold. The wild's are cold: youth purple, adult blue, legend turquoise. Every baby's is pink on either side. This is drawing only; no rule reads it. Code: `src/render/leash/ranks.ts#rankKnobs`, `config/tuning.json#leash.ranks.colours`, `config/tuning.json#leash.ranks.enemy`, `config/tuning.json#colourGrammar`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "the forest is cold, the party is warm".
2. `leash.ranks.chain` only draws the chains (link sizes, how a travelling follower's chain hangs from its sigil). The chains' rules are `leash.chain`. Code: `src/render/leash/chain.ts#ChainMotion`, `config/tuning.json#leash.ranks.chain.fall`, `config/decisions/1682-leash-chains.json#decisions`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02: "fall to the ground quickly and become their walking path".

## Babies' queue on the leash

1. On the ground her babies follow in single file along where she has walked, the stack's bottom baby first. Each keeps clear of the one in front by their sizes, never under 5 m (`leash.babies.gap`). Code: `src/rules/babies.ts#queueDistances`, `src/rules/babies.ts#queueGap`, `src/rules/leash.ts#trailPoint`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.gap`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "The spacing in this queue should adjust depending on how large the babies are (some are quite large and so smush together in the queue)".
2. While anything within 30 m is going for her or one of hers, the babies scatter 55 m or more away, on land. They stay scattered until 3 s after the last attacker. Code: `src/rules/babies.ts#attackerNear`, `src/rules/babies.ts#scatterSpot`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.scatter`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.calm`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "they should scatter further, e.g. outside of my camera view".
3. A baby has no chain. It never grounds her, drags her, fights or gets hit. More than 35 m from her it runs at 40 m/s. Code: `src/rules/chains.ts#chained`, `src/rules/babies.ts#stepBaby`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.farAt`, `config/tuning.json#leash.babies.farSpeed`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04: "No animals should attack babies".

## Losing followers

1. One of hers beaten in a fight (its ring full) runs off to the beach. It is off her leash and its sigil is gone for the rest of the night. Code: `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#w.loseParty(o.id)`, `src/rules/game.ts#loseParty`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04: "it's sad when animals die".
2. Knocked out (4 hits, one back every 20 s out of the fight), she loses nobody. Every sigil on her stack falls at once onto the free hex cells nearest where she went down, and each creature stays hers, parked there. Sigils already placed stay. Code: `src/rules/knockout.ts#fallAtKnockdown`, `src/rules/knockout.ts#stepKnockout`, `config/tuning.json#knockout.releaseEach`, `config/tuning.json#witchHealth.hits`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-03: "If you're knocked down, animals' sigils fall and stay where you were knocked down".
3. Losing a soundsystem takes none of her leashed or parked creatures. All of them are let go only when the party is over (they sleep where they stand) or the night is won (they join the conga). Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#startPartyOver`, `src/rules/win.ts#W.leash.stack = []`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "When conga animals are following me, they shouldn't be leashed to me - they just follow me".

## Relic flasks

1. A flask is never carried. E while she stands on its sigil (within 5.625 m; the sigil lies 6 m south of the bottle) drinks it on the spot. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#flaskUnder(relicsOut(g)`, `src/rules/legends.ts#flaskUnder`, `config/legends.json#relics.sigilOffset`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18: "All flasks give growth spurt (all creatures following you become adults)".
2. Drinking it turns every baby and youth on her stack into an adult at once. Parked creatures and legends are not touched. With nobody to grow, the flask stays and the press only fizzles. The new adults ground her (see "Chains and what grounds her"). Code: `src/rules/berries.ts#growthSpurt`, `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#flaskDrunk`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-18: "all creatures following you become adults".
3. There are no flasks before the party spell is cast, and a legend is no longer made happy by one. The code that carried a relic's sigil in the stack is gone; `leash.relics` stays as an always-empty field for saved nights. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#relicsOut`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "Relics, like finds, only appear once the party starts".
