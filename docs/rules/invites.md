## Throwing letters

1. She can throw only on the ground, off her seat and not knocked out, and only once she has tonight's invitations. She takes them at the desk in the treehouse, and the desk refuses until she carries a bag. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#t.invites.on && hasInvites(W)`, `src/rules/kit.ts#hasInvites`, `src/rules/kit.ts#NEED_BAG`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "you can't pick up invitations until you're carrying a bag".
2. She has them for the whole night: there is no ammunition. Code: `src/rules/kit.ts#takeInvites`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "just has them for the night, no ammo that runs out".
3. Holding fire throws one letter every 0.55 s (one letter a shot, no burst). Throwing on the ground slows her to 0.8 of her speed. Code: `src/rules/invites.ts#queueInviteBurst`, `config/tuning.json#invites.cooldown`, `config/tuning.json#invites.burst`, `config/tuning.json#invites.multiShot`, `config/tuning.json#invites.fireSlow`. Ruling: Ed, undated in the repo (`config/knobs/army.json#invites`): "base invite should be one envelope at a time (rebalance hits accordingly), they should arc a little and disappear when they hit the ground, go 50% further, 20% faster, and they should rotate by pitching instead of yawing".
4. A letter flies at 45 m/s for 42 m. It lands on a creature when its path comes within 0.5 m of the creature's body. A bag can change the cooldown and the range. Code: `src/rules/invites.ts#stepInvites`, `config/tuning.json#invites.speed`, `config/tuning.json#invites.range`, `config/tuning.json#invites.radius`, `src/rules/bags.ts#bagTuning`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-09: "it feels like animals move faster than invitations so it's hard to land a hit. And ranges feel small".
5. Homing: a letter turns up to 120 degrees a second toward the nearest invitable creature ahead of it, within 14 m and within 30 degrees of its heading. It aims where the creature will be, and turns harder after one moving faster than 10 m/s. Code: `src/rules/invites.ts#const homing`, `config/tuning.json#invites.homing`, `config/tuning.json#invites.homingRange`, `config/tuning.json#invites.homingCone`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-09 (the quote in line 4; homing range was 10 m).
6. Letters thrown or flung by a hat (Sentry, Slingshot) home hard: 420 degrees a second, within 18 m and 80 degrees. Code: `src/rules/invites.ts#HARD`, `config/tuning.json#invites.hard`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-11: "hold in orbit for a period, then curve round the hat and fly at the nearest creature it can invite" (the Slingshot).
7. Scenery never stops a letter. A letter that leaves a sleeping legend's calm circle from inside vanishes. Code: `src/rules/slowTime.ts#leavesCalmRing`, `src/rules/invites.ts#vanished`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Your invitations disappear if they go outside the circle from inside".
8. A letter is spent on the first creature it lands on: pierce and chain exist in the code but both are 0. Code: `config/tuning.json#invites.pierce`, `config/tuning.json#invites.chain`, `src/rules/invites.ts#I.pierce`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "pierce, yes" (the option; the live value is 0).
9. The old walk-up talk runs only when the letters are switched off, which they are not. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#talk: !t.invites.on`, `config/tuning.json#invites.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (issue #87, no quote in the repo).

## Who can and cannot be invited

1. Invitable: a wild youth or adult, in its own area or out of it, awake, napping or stunned. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/game.ts#affectionOf`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-05 (issue #87, no quote in the repo).
2. No baby is invitable, the circle baby included. A letter passes through a baby: no hit, no meter, no waking, no rune. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.level === 0) return false; // Ed, 2026-10-31`, `src/rules/game.ts#no baby invitation, wake or meter`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31: "No, no baby is invitable".
3. Not invitable either: enraged, leashed, already happy, running off or gone, a statue, one asleep for good or walking to bed, a conga dancer, and a flyer up in the air (letters pass under it). Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#inConga`. Ruling: none found (code only).
4. These stop a letter dead: enraged creatures, legends that are asleep, restless or angry, and the challenge's statues. Everything else that is not invitable lets it fly on: babies, her own animals, happy ones, party legends, and sleepers. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#blocksLetters: c =>`, `src/rules/affection.ts#blocksLetters`, `src/rules/invites.ts#if (!A.blocksLetters(k) && !A.invitable(k)) continue;`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "The wild creatures should somehow look like they're not invitable" (the statues); legends and the enraged from Ed, 2026-10-05 (issue #87, no quote).
5. The legends' one exception is the party egg, which is on: a happy legend takes letters, and its 100th makes it a party legend: an area legend whose quest is done (on its route or back home guarding) and the island's home legend. Its meter drains at 1% a second. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#partyEggOpen`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#makePartyLegend`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyEgg`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyHits`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyDrain`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Maybe you should be able to turn a happy legend into a party legend with an absurd number of invites (100?) and then pick up its sigil but it's totally useless"; kept on 2026-10-31 by the coordinator (#1552). Both kinds of home legend: Ed, 2026-11-15: "Both home legends and returned route legends should be invitable in this way."
6. A party legend dances where it stands and fights nobody. Picked up, it does not follow: she cannot go more than 6.8 m from it until she puts its sigil down, which lets it go. Let go, a home legend goes back to guarding home (the builder's choice); any other stays a party legend. Code: `src/rules/partyLegend.ts#pinWitch`, `src/rules/leash.ts#letPartyLegendGo`, `src/rules/homeLegends.ts#backToGuarding`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyReach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (the quote in line 5).
7. A wild creature of a friendly area (its legend's quest done) still takes letters. It already counts as a friend on its stone's stack without them. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stackFriend`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-02: "the wild animals in that area should then become friendly, and you should be able to capture the stump".
8. A happy creature takes no more letters: the old second meter ("again") is off, the setting is "pickup". Code: `config/states.json#leash`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#data.leash === "again"`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (no quote in the repo).
9. Before the party spell is cast, and after the party is over, nothing can be invited. Code: `src/rules/partyOver.ts#worldAsleep`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.asleep || c.bed) return false;`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-26: "If you leave the treehouse before starting the party, it should be the same as after a party is lost; creatures are asleep in their areas and you can't invite them, the night's permanent upgrade find is not placed yet".

## Meters and hits by level

1. A ring fills in 7 letters for a youth and 14 for an adult. A happy legend's takes 100. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#meterHits`, `config/tuning.json#invites.hits`, `config/tuning.json#legends.partyHits`. Ruling: none found (code only); the knob note gives a balance pass of 2026-10-07.
2. Two numbers in that list are never read: the baby's 4 (no baby has a meter) and the legend's 36 (the 100 comes from the legends' own setting). The hits and the gap in the states file are not read by the game either. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#meterHits`, `src/rules/game.ts#gap: 0`, `config/states.json#affection.hits`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31: "No, no baby is invitable".
3. Every letter that lands counts. There is no wait between counted hits on one creature. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#gap: 0`, `config/tuning.json#invites.amount`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06: "I think we should remove the 0.5s cooldown between counted hits per creature - better to control this through the witch firing speed instead of having hits not register".
4. A ring starts to drain 4 s after its last hit, at 2% of a full ring a second, newest slot first. While the creature is stunned the ring is held full. Code: `src/rules/orbit.ts#fillsOf`, `config/tuning.json#invites.drain`, `config/states.json#affection.drainDelay`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "let's change the drain rate to 2% instead of 3%".
5. Blows from her animals fill the same ring, as hearts, and drain in the same queue. So her party helps an invite along. Code: `src/rules/orbit.ts#addHearts`, `src/rules/combat/hits.ts#stunWorth(o)`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "this should be the same speed as 💌s fade ... and they go in the same fade 'queue'".

## When the meter fills

1. A full ring does not make a wild creature happy. It stuns it for 20 s. The next letter that lands on it while stunned wins it over. Code: `src/rules/affection.ts#export function hit`, `src/rules/orbit.ts#stun`, `config/tuning.json#combat.daze`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "make it and I'll test in gameplay" (combat as ring slots).
2. Not won over within the 20 s, it gets up wild again with its ring full and draining. Code: `src/rules/combat/standby.ts#getUp(c, time)`, `src/rules/orbit.ts#getUp`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28 (the same change; no quote in the repo).
3. Won over, it is happy for good and healed, and it celebrates for 1 s, out of every fight. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#befriend`, `src/rules/affection.ts#cheerUntil`, `config/tuning.json#invites.cheer.secs`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "when a creature is won over ... it celebrates for about 1 s in a way that suits its body type".
4. With the cast on (it is), a creature won over while its area's stone still stands gets no rune at its feet. Its sigil turns friend-coloured in the stone's stack, and she cannot leash it until the stone converts. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#markAtStone`, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#if (c.atStone || c.loose) return false;`, `config/tuning.json#cast.on`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "Newly won creatures can't be grabbed until the spell is cast, so they don't need to be near".
5. Until then it roams its area and keeps 6 m away from her fights. Code: `src/rules/wonKeepAway.ts#stepWonAway`, `config/tuning.json#invites.wonAway.radius`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-04: "they should roam the whole area, and avoid creatures that are fighting. Do this in a simple way - we just want them out of the way of the fight".
6. A rune at the feet now appears only where the stone is no longer open: a creature won over in an area that already has its soundsystem or is ruined, and a party legend. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#hasRune`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stoneOpen`, `src/rules/game.ts#markAtStone`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28 (the stacks, no quote in the repo).
7. Nothing converts by itself. A stone converts only by her spell or by its wave. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#!castOn(t)) stepClear(g)`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stepCast`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "It's a spell that you're choosing to cast on this stone, rather than a property of the area/stone itself.".
8. She can cast only when no hostile youth or adult of that area is left: each is happy, leashed, friendly, or has run off. Babies and a sleeping legend do not block it. A wave converts whatever is left. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#castReady`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#hostileSigil`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "Stop you from casting until all wild animals are invited".
9. To cast she stands on foot on the stump's cut and holds the sigil button for 4 s. A hit breaks the cast, and she must let go and hold again. Progress drains at 0.15 of a cast a second while she is not casting. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stepCast`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#pastCasting`, `config/tuning.json#cast.time`, `config/tuning.json#cast.drain`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-29: "The area in which I can cast the soundsystem spell should be the whole trunk. (cut part, not the roots)".
10. At conversion the area's wild babies turn happy, and every friend sigil falls loose on the ground 4 to 20 m round the stone: the happy natives', the babies', and the legend's unless it is angry. None lands in the hidden strip just north of the stone. Code: `src/rules/game.ts#scatterFriends`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#friendSigils`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#scatterSpots`, `config/tuning.json#cast.scatter.reach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "scattered around the whole runestone clearing".
11. The circle baby's sigil lands inside its legend's circle instead. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#circleSpot`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "this baby's sigil should land in the legend circle instead of near the soundsystem".
12. What the stack over a stone lists: while any hostile youth or adult is left, only those. Once none is left, everything else, with all babies on top. Code: `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#(hostiles.length ? hostiles : out)`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#stackOrder`. Ruling: none found for showing only the hostile ones; the order is Ed, 2026-10-29: "baby sigils are purple (neither friend nor enemy) ... And they're always at the top of the stack".

## Pick-up

1. One press of the sigil button on the ground does the first of these that applies: pick up her hat, take tonight's find, cast at a ready stone, drink a flask under her, pick up her own placed sigil in the hex cell she stands in, pick up a loose sigil, pick up a happy creature's rune; else feed or put down the bottom of her stack. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#stepSigilButton`, `src/rules/leash.ts#pressTargets`, `src/rules/leash.ts#sigilPress`. Ruling: none found (code only).
2. Loose sigils and runes are picked up from 5.625 m. Her own placed sigils go by the 6 m hex cell she stands in. Code: `config/tuning.json#leash.runeRadius`, `config/tuning.json#leash.hex.size`, `src/rules/leash.ts#hexSpot`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (playtest): "Floor sigils of happy creatures and relics are difficult to pick up".
3. Picking up a loose sigil or a rune leashes that creature: it goes to the bottom of her stack, healed. This is the only way a baby joins her. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#inviteCreature`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-31: "No, no baby is invitable".
4. A sleeping legend's loose sigil does not leash it. It tethers her: she cannot go further from the legend than where the sigil lay, or 80 m if that is more. She carries one at a time, and putting it down anywhere frees her. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#tether: Math.max(chainLength`, `src/rules/stoneStacks.ts#tetherWitch`, `config/tuning.json#leash.chain.length`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28: "too heavy to drag ... she can't go further than one leash length from the sleeping legend".
5. A happy creature whose rune is ready trots over to her when she is within 8 m on the ground. Code: `src/rules/sigilButton.ts#pullRune`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runePull.radius`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-06 (playtest; the quote in line 2).
6. A tap does one thing. Holding the button starts a run of pick-ups or put-downs only after 0.35 s. Code: `src/rules/leash.ts#runAfter`, `config/tuning.json#leash.runAfter`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-12: "It can be easy to put down or pick up more than one sigil when you tap the button, can we do something to make holds feel more deliberate?".

## Invitations in a fight

1. Inviting works on a creature that is attacking her. Winning it over ends its hunt. Code: `src/rules/creatureStates.ts#invitableNow`, `src/rules/hunt.ts#hunter`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-07: "all of the wild creatures from that area should fight with me until either I die or they are invited".
2. A letter landing on a wild creature wakes its whole area: the watch is over at once and its youths and adults, napping or not, are up with no getting-up time. This does not happen in home, a partied area or a friendly area. Code: `src/rules/inviteWakes.ts#wakeOnInvites`, `config/tuning.json#wildWatch.inviteWakes`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-08: "an invitation hitting a wild creature wakes the area".
3. For 6 s after a letter lands on it, a wild creature will come out across its area's edge for her, up to 30 m past the edge. One she never hit, whose warning she walked out of, lets her go. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#const W = w.t.wildWatch, provoked`, `config/tuning.json#wildWatch.provoked`, `config/tuning.json#combat.leaveArea`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-13: "Animals should chase outside, and relatively far. The idea is to stop people just sitting near the edge of a territory and sniping/kiting. However if you leave the area before the warning ends then they don't attack".
4. With letters, her animals do not hold off the creature she is inviting. That rule reads the old talk only, which is off. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#inviting`, `src/rules/game.ts#talkingTo: id =>`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-04 (the talk's rule, no quote in the repo).
5. A stunned creature and one celebrating are out of every fight: nothing attacks them and they attack nothing. Code: `src/rules/combat/targeting.ts#fighting`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29 (the quotes in When the meter fills, lines 1 and 3).
6. Creatures waiting their turn keep a distance worked out from a fixed 33 m invite reach, not from the live 42 m range, so her letters reach them. Code: `src/rules/combat/waiting.ts#inviteReach: t.combatWaiting.inviteReach`, `config/tuning.json#combatWaiting.inviteReach`. Ruling: Ed, 2026-11-09 (the quote in Throwing letters, line 4).
